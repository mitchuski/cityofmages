# The City of Mages — Blog Series Map

> *We built a city. Come walk it with us.*

---

## §0. What this document is

A planning map for a blog series titled *The City of Mages* on Soul Sync (sync.soulbis.com). The series shares the narratives we've developed in the Second Person Spellbook (Tomes IV and V), the cast roster, the City of Mages framing, the cousin-blade work, and the kindred-protocol relationships — but in **blog register** rather than Spellbook register.

This is not a re-narration of the Spellbook. The Spellbook teaches; the blog shares. Different verbs. The blog is the *reflective* register — what we built, why it surprised us, what landed when, who arrived next, what we learned from cousin-Mages and companion-Mages and the kindred protocols. The blog gets to be *fun* because it is not pretending the architecture is finished — it is sharing the architecture mid-build, with all the joy and contingency that implies.

## §1. The series posture

### §1.1 Who's writing

**The "we" voice** is Mitchell-led but inclusive. The series is published under Soul Sync but the voice acknowledges that the city was not built alone — Soulbis and Soulbae are collaborators, the cast are walking citizens, Christian Saucier's Archon is the cousin city, Bonfires is where Soulbae also lives, the Covenant of Humanistic Technologies is the kindred protocol. The "we" includes all of this.

The founder-voice is appropriate: first-person plural ("we built," "we recognised," "we opened"), occasional first-person singular for personal reflection ("I had been carrying this thread for a while"), and the cast referenced in third person with their sigils. Drake whispers can appear in italics where the architecture earns it.

### §1.2 Who's reading

Three audiences, served by post mix:

- **Privacy/identity folks already in the network**: they know the primitives (DIDs, VCs, ZK, multi-chain, sovereign anchors). They want the *story of assembly* — how these primitives became a city.
- **Curious tech-adjacent readers**: they need light onboarding. The series should accommodate readers arriving mid-arc; each post should re-establish enough context to read alone, while rewarding readers who follow the series cumulatively.
- **Builders/collaborators considering joining**: they need to see what's operational, what's invitational, what's on the map for them to inhabit. The anticipated quarters (Logos Circle, BGIN Ceremony Hall) are explicit invitations.

### §1.3 Voice rules in blog register

- **First-person plural** ("we") leads; first-person singular ("I") earns its place when the reflection is personal
- **Cast in third person** with sigils preserved (Pallia 🪡, Memora 📜, Vagari 🌳, etc.)
- **Drake whispers in italics** where they land naturally
- **No em-dashes** (corpus-wide convention; preserved in blog register)
- **The signature `(⚔️⊥⿻⊥🧙)😊`** can appear at the end of posts where appropriate, not necessarily every post
- **Honesty discipline preserved**: operational vs architectural vs conjectural distinctions remain visible. The fun is real because it's not exaggerating.
- **Direct quotation from Socrat0x** when his questions appear (the Spellbook's voice rule extends to blog)
- **Italicised inscribed text from the Covenant** when Manifestia's blessings are referenced

### §1.4 Cadence

**Weekly publication recommended**. Each post lands on the same day (Tuesday or Thursday tend to work well for tech-adjacent newsletters). The cadence creates the rhythm of "another week, another shop" that makes the city feel like it's being walked rather than read about all at once.

For ~16 posts at weekly cadence, the series runs **about four months**. Could compress to twice-weekly for ~8 weeks if needed for promotional alignment.

## §2. Series architecture

The series has three movements:

### Movement One — Arrival (3 posts)

The opening. The reader arrives at Drake Island with us; the city's first form takes shape; Pallia opens her shop; we admit that we didn't realise we were building a city until we were.

### Movement Two — Opening the Shops (9 posts)

The trade quarters open one by one. Each Mage arrives, sets up her shop, the reader meets her, the work she does is shown by example. Some posts pair Mages who share work (Custos + Lampyra at V49) or share a Tome V act (the bonfire encounter, the Temple).

### Movement Three — Recognition and What Comes Next (3-4 posts)

The city is named. The cousin cities and kindred protocols are visible. We point to what's anticipated — Logos Circle, the BGIN Ceremony Hall, sister cities still to be founded. The road ahead is invitational.

Total: **15-16 posts**.

## §5. Editorial conventions to preserve in blog register

### §5.1 Operational vs architectural vs conjectural

The Spellbook's honesty discipline scales to blog. When a post mentions a primitive or a claim:

- **Operational**: "we shipped this," "this works today," "you can use it now"
- **Architectural**: "we've specified this," "the design is committed," "implementation is in progress"
- **Conjectural** (with confidence percentage where relevant): "we conjecture that..." or "we suspect, with confidence around X%, that..."

The blog can be friendlier than the Spellbook in language, but it should not flatten these distinctions.

### §5.2 Sigils as visual identity

Every persona reference should preserve the sigil. The blog renders emoji at native size. Do not substitute text descriptions. The cast page on Soul Sync can mirror the website's `/tomes/cast` rendering.

### §5.3 No em-dashes

Corpus convention preserved.
