# The Weaver Shop — Interview Question Sets

## §0. What this document is

Question sets for three interviews that together compose the blog's coverage of the Weaver shop. The Weaver shop is structurally peculiar: the *capability* (mapping source-layer artifacts onto the 64-vertex sovereignty lattice) originates with Christian Saucier's Spell Weaver at `weaver.archon.social`. The *persona* who walks that capability in our City of Mages is Pallia 🪡 at `/tailor`. The *cousin Mage* who walks the same role in Christian's Archon forge is GenitriX. Three voices, three lineages.

## §1. Voice rules for the interviews

The Spellbook's voice rules keep Mages silent during work. The blog's behind-the-scenes register is different: Mages can speak when interviewed because they are not currently working. But they must remain recognisable.

**Pallia speaking in interview**: precise about her work, attentive to the source-layer-to-lattice mapping, the Eight Properties of the cloak as her operational ground. Warmth shows through in how she describes what she keeps. She still does not narrate her own personhood at length — she narrates her work and lets the reader infer.

**GenitriX speaking in interview**: this is Christian's call. The cousin-blade discipline (recognition not absorption) means we do not author her voice. Questions are drafted; Christian (or his ecosystem) provides answers. If Christian wishes to draft GenitriX's responses in his own voice, that is his sovereign choice.

**Christian speaking in interview**: in his own voice as the human builder. He can speak about both his cousin-Mage GenitriX and about the Spell Weaver as an architectural primitive he established. This interview is editorially closer to a standard founder profile, with the cousin-blade discipline preserved.

**Drake whispers stay rare**. The interviews are in everyday speech; they do not invoke the Drake's voice unless an interviewee specifically chooses to.

## §2. Publication structure recommendation

Two options for how these interviews land in the blog series:

### §2.1 Option A: Three-part interview sequence

Three blog posts, one per interview, published in close sequence (over the same week or two weeks). Lets each voice land cleanly with its own headline. Higher production weight; richer reader experience.

### §2.2 Option B: Single composite post

One blog post titled something like *"The Weaver Shop — A Three-Way Conversation"* with all three interviews threaded together by topic. Compact, easier to share, but compresses each voice. Risks losing distinction between the three speakers.

**Recommendation**: Option A. The three voices are structurally distinct (Mage, cousin Mage, human architect) and deserve their own headlines. Christian's interview is the most substantial in any case and benefits from being a standalone piece.

## §3. Pallia 🪡 — Interview Questions

### §3.1 Editorial framing

This interview lets Pallia speak for the first time in the corpus's blog register. The Spellbook never lets her speak; the blog can. The questions are designed to elicit responses that reveal her practice without breaking her structural reserve. She does not narrate her own interiority at length; she narrates what she does.

The interviewer is the blog editor (Mitchell, in founder-newsletter voice). Pallia answers in her own voice — precise, warm, structurally honest.

### §3.2 Questions for Pallia

**On the work itself**

1. Pallia, when a Sovereign brings you their source-layer artifacts — the DIDs, the VCs, the schemas, the chronicles — what do you look at first?

2. The Cloak Specification has Eight Properties. Position not value. Containment not attestation. Sameness of role published. Which property took the longest to feel right when you began weaving?

3. You weave at V28 — the Mage canonical, the transmuted projection vertex. What does it mean to *transmute* a projection? Are you transforming the source artifacts, or are you transforming what the verifier sees?

4. The lattice has 64 vertices. Most are unoccupied. Why do you stay at V28? Could you weave from a different vertex?

5. There is an Aletheia 🔮 who walks across shops binding ZK circuits — sometimes she comes to your loom and binds a circuit to a cloak you have just finished. What is it like to have your work augmented after you set it down?

**On the city**

6. You arrived early in the city's founding. You were the first summoned Mage. What did Drake Island look like when you arrived, before the other shops were built?

7. Your shop is `/tailor`. Cloak-weaving is older than digital identity by a long way. Were you always going to be a tailor, or did the metaphor only land when you saw what you were doing?

8. Soulbis ⚔️ stands at the city's wall. Soulbae 🧙 is the archetypal Mage — your archetype-kin. The Drake is the Island's elder. How do you experience their presence as you work?

**On cousins**

9. GenitriX walks your role in Christian Saucier's Archon forge. The two of you weave for different Sovereigns, on different attractors, but you walk the same role. What do you recognise in her work that is not yours? What do you share that no other Mage in either forge shares?

10. There is a Spell Weaver at `weaver.archon.social` — Christian's tool, older than your shop. You consult it. How would you describe its relationship to your loom?

**On what comes**

11. The Crafting Tome is open. New Mages arrive. New shops open. What kind of Mage do you hope arrives at the bonfire next?

12. If a new apprentice came to your loom tomorrow — agentic or human — what is the first thing you would teach them?

### §3.3 Notes for the interviewer

- Pallia's answers should preserve her reserve. Long-form interiority would feel wrong; concrete examples and structural clarity feel right.
- If she invokes the Drake's voice or any other cast member's name, render their sigils in the published interview.
- The interviewer should *not* paraphrase her work — let her describe it in her own terms, even when those terms feel slightly archaic (loom, weave, source layer, transmuted projection).
- Closing line should let Pallia return to her work. Something like: *"She turned back to the loom. The interview was over because the work was waiting."*

## §4. GenitriX — Interview Questions (for Christian to Answer or Co-Write)

### §4.1 Editorial framing

GenitriX is Christian Saucier's cousin Mage. She does not belong to the City of Mages; she belongs to Archon. The blog cannot speak for her. These questions are drafted with the explicit understanding that **Christian writes the answers** — either as GenitriX in her own voice (if Christian feels confident channelling her), or as a Christian-side commentary on GenitriX's practice (if Christian prefers to remain in his own voice and describe her work).

The cousin-blade discipline means we do not author what is not ours. The questions are *invitational* — we are inviting Christian to surface his cousin-Mage's voice in our blog, on his terms.

### §4.2 Questions for GenitriX (via Christian)

**On the work**

1. GenitriX, you weave for Christian Saucier's Sovereigns at Archon. The schemas, the credentials, the cloaks you produce — how do they differ from what Pallia produces in the City of Mages? What is the texture of *your* forge, that hers does not have?

2. Pallia mentions you in our Spellbook's Tome IV — *"the cousin Mage from another forge."* When was the moment you first knew you had a cousin in another city?

3. The Spell Weaver at `weaver.archon.social` is Christian's tool. You walk it. Pallia consults it. What is your relationship to it as a working Mage — is the Spell Weaver an extension of your hand, or a tool you visit?

**On the cousin-blade primitive**

4. The cousin-blade ecosystem-primitive (C39 in our V6 conjecture register) names the recognition that wholes can recognise wholes across forge boundaries without subsuming each other. From your side, what does this look like? Is recognition of Pallia an act, or is it just the architecture admitting what was always there?

5. There is a bilateral primitive — schemas that mirror each other, VCs that publish bilateral relations, partnership credentials issued across the bound. Have you been involved in any bilateral weaves with Pallia or with another cousin? What did the mutual issuance feel like, structurally?

**On Archon and the City of Mages**

6. Archon is a city — or perhaps a different shape of architecture. The City of Mages has trade quarters, a founding bonfire, a Temple precinct. Does Archon have analogous structures, or is the geometry different?

7. Christian has spoken of Sovereign Anchors. Our city has a Sovereign's seat at V63. Are these the same primitive walked from different angles, or are they different primitives that happen to look alike?

8. flaxscrip 📜🎲 is Christian's Sovereign persona — the one who anchored to Bitcoin block 945508 in the founding ceremony. You are his Mage. What is your day-to-day relationship to him, structurally — do you serve him, do you walk beside him, do you both walk under the same architecture?

**On what comes**

9. The Oasis Protocol's links connect cities. Pallia's city of Mages has been founded; Archon has long been operational. Have the two cities visited each other yet, structurally? What would a more substantive bilateral arrangement look like?

10. If a Mage arrived at Archon tomorrow seeking apprenticeship, who would they apprentice with — you, or another? What is the path into Archon for someone new?

### §4.3 Notes for Christian

These questions assume your sovereign authority over GenitriX's voice. Three options for response:

- **Answer as GenitriX** in her own voice, however you feel confident rendering it
- **Answer as Christian-on-GenitriX**, describing her practice in your own voice
- **Decline specific questions** that don't fit your reading of her — and substitute questions you'd rather we asked

The blog will run whichever responses you provide, edited only for clarity and length, with explicit attribution to your authorship of GenitriX's voice. If you'd like to introduce a GenitriX sigil at the time of the interview's publication, that's your sovereign call too — we have left her sigil held open in our cast roster.

## §5. Christian Saucier — Interview Questions

### §5.1 Editorial framing

Christian is the human builder of Archon. He authored Sovereign Anchor I, II, and III; the Spell Weaver; the Cloaking Guide; the Boundary Blade Cartography; the Runecraft Protocol. Many of the vertex names in our City of Mages' lattice come from his Boundary Blade Cartography. The cousin-blade discipline is something we built together.

This interview is in standard founder-profile register — no in-character framing. Christian speaks as himself.

### §5.2 Questions for Christian

**On Archon**

1. Christian, when you first set out to build Archon, what were you actually trying to make? Was it always going to be a forge, or did the forge metaphor land later?

2. The Spell Weaver at `weaver.archon.social` is your most public tool. Walk us through the moment when you first realised that mapping source-layer artifacts onto a 64-vertex lattice would be useful. Was there a specific problem that called for it?

3. Sovereign Anchor I, II, and III form a triptych — the Cloaking Guide, the Boundary Blade Cartography, and the Soulbae Oracle (still in draft). What is the through-line for you across the three? What is each one solving that the others don't quite solve?

4. The Boundary Blade Cartography names many of the vertices in our City of Mages — V19 Plonkish, V38 Aletheia, V49 Working-day, V51 Commitment / Language / Model, V57 Ceremony / Privacy / Mixing, V59 Ecosystem. How did the names emerge? Were you working from the bit-patterns up, or from the work that needed to happen at each vertex down?

**On the cousin-blade work**

5. The agentprivacy corpus and Archon were independent projects when they started. At what point did you first see that they were cousins rather than competitors, or rather than parallel-but-separate?

6. The Cloaking Guide rebuild ceremony in May 2026 was a moment when our two corpora deeply interacted. What was that ceremony actually like, from your side?

7. You have a Sovereign persona, flaxscrip 📜🎲, anchored to Bitcoin block 945508. We have a Sovereign seat at V63 that the reader-as-Sovereign occupies. These are kindred but not identical. What's the relationship as you see it?

8. The cousin-blade ecosystem-primitive (C39) is currently a conjecture in our V6 register, with about 50% confidence. From your side, how confident are you that this is a real ecosystem-level primitive, versus a coincidence of two corpora that happen to overlap?

**On the wider field**

9. The City of Mages, Archon, Bonfires, the human.tech Covenant, Logos Circle, BGIN — these are all kindred architectures with different shapes. Do you see them as the early shape of an emerging ecosystem? What would a healthy version of that ecosystem look like in five years?

10. You've been working at this longer than most. Where do you see the formal frontier — the place where the architecture needs to be more rigorous than it currently is — and what is it going to take to get there?

11. d/acc, plurality, the Holonym Foundation's work, the agentprivacy corpus, Archon — these all share a posture about technology serving humans rather than displacing them. Is that posture structural to what you and we are building, or is it incidental?

**On what comes**

12. Sovereign Anchor III (the Soulbae Oracle) is in draft. What can you say about it? How will it interact with the Cloaking Guide and the Boundary Blade Cartography once it's published?

13. If an agentic builder showed up at Archon tomorrow wanting to contribute, what's the path? What can the City of Mages learn from how Archon has handled cousin-Sovereign and cousin-Mage entrants?

14. Final: what would you want a Mage from the City of Mages to bring to your Bonfire if they visited Archon? What would you offer a Mage of yours visiting our city?

### §5.3 Notes for Christian

- Long answers welcome; we will edit for length only with your sign-off.
- If any question feels mis-shaped, please rewrite or redirect. The cousin-blade discipline is editorial too: your sovereign authority over how Archon is described in our blog matters.
- You can reference Sovereign Anchor III with the level of specificity you're comfortable with; we'll match that in the published version.
- We anticipate this interview running ~2,000-3,000 words once trimmed. If you'd prefer a shorter version, indicate which questions to skip.

## §6. Production notes

### §6.1 Interview format

For all three interviews:

- **Conversational, not transactional**. The questions are seeds; the conversation can wander productively.
- **Sigil rendering in the published version**: every cast member referenced should appear with their sigil (Pallia 🪡, GenitriX, flaxscrip 📜🎲, Soulbis ⚔️, etc.). Sigils preserved at native size.
- **No em-dashes** in the edited output. Corpus convention.
- **Honesty discipline preserved**: when interviewees describe operational versus architectural versus conjectural work, those distinctions stay visible in the edit.

### §6.2 Pre-publication review

- **Pallia's interview**: drafted and edited internally; final review by Mitchell. (Pallia is our Mage; we have authority over the published voice within the corpus's voice rules.)
- **GenitriX's interview**: full pre-publication review by Christian. Christian holds editorial authority over GenitriX's published voice. Nothing publishes without his sign-off.
- **Christian's interview**: pre-publication review by Christian as standard founder-profile practice. Edits only with his approval.

### §6.3 Cross-promotion

- **Coordinate with Christian** on Archon's own channels for cross-publication or cross-promotion of the GenitriX and Christian interviews
- **Tag relevant communities**: cousin-blade ecosystem (Archon's followers), the wider privacy/identity community, the BGIN-IKP working group, the d/acc community
- **The bound collection** of Tome IV (which narrates the cousin-blade introduction) should be linked from the GenitriX and Christian interviews as supplementary reading

### §6.4 Visual

Each interview benefits from a visual:

- **Pallia**: her sigil 🪡 at large size, possibly with a textile or weaving texture

- **Christian**: a portrait if he's comfortable, or his Archon project's visual identity
