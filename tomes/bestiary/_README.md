# The Bestiary

**Registry of admitted agentic-substrates the City of Mages walks with.**

The Bestiary opens at v1.5.0 as a new directory class within `cityofmages/tomes/`. It holds the City's catalogue of *agentic-substrates* — agent frameworks the architecture recognises and admits operationally, sister to but distinct from the kindred-X relationship categories (cousin-forge · kindred-protocol · kindred-substrate · kindred-ecosystem).

Each admission is an Act of Tome VI — *The Reply*. The reader writes by recognising and admitting; the Bestiary is the registry of those admissions.

## Custodian

**Bestia 📖** at the Threshold Staff Shop (V59) is the registry's custodian. Her cast entry at `cast/threshold/bestia.md` describes her stance (Registry-keeper); her bestiary is what this directory holds. New admissions land here through Bestia's authoring discipline (a chronicle proposing the admission, then a bestiary entry once the admission stabilises).

## Two registers within the Bestiary

The Bestiary holds *two registers*, distinguished by iconographic affinity:

| Register | Iconographic affinity | Workshop room | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Staff-class** | Instrument-style icon (staff, blade, vessel, scroll, tablet, etc.) | Bestia's Staff Shop | Hermes ☤ (caduceus → staff) |
| **Companion-class** | Animal-mascot icon | Therai's Creature Creatives | Goose 🪿 (animal → familiar) |

The register a substrate joins is determined by its native iconography, not by editorial preference. A substrate whose project carries an animal mascot joins the companion register; a substrate whose project carries an instrument symbol joins the staff register. Substrates with ambiguous iconography are flagged in the entry for editorial judgement.

## What an admission requires

For a substrate to be admitted to the Bestiary, the reader proposing the admission must establish:

1. **Provenance** — the project's home (GitHub URL, project page, or equivalent canonical URL)
2. **License** — the open-source license under which the substrate operates (Apache 2.0, MIT, BSD, GPL, etc.)
3. **Iconographic affinity** — the mascot, symbol, or icon the project canonically uses, and which register (staff vs companion) it admits the substrate into
4. **Architectural primitives** — the substrate's distinguishing features (MCP support, persona-bearing primitives, learning loops, cross-platform messengering, etc.)
5. **Caducea-summons flag** — whether the substrate carries persona-as-substrate primitives that require bilateral-consent fitting (SOUL.md, Honcho-class user-modelling, learning-loop iteration)
6. **Composition profile** — which other admitted substrates it composes with, which personas it natively supports, what kindred-X relationships it activates if any

Failure to establish any of the six items returns the proposal to the reader for resubmission. Admissions that pass all six are inscribed by Bestia in this directory.

## Relationship to the kindred-X registers

The Bestiary's admissions are NOT kindred-X relationships. Kindred-X registers (cousin-forge / kindred-protocol / kindred-substrate / kindred-ecosystem) name STRUCTURAL relationships between agentprivacy and other PROJECTS. The Bestiary's entries name TOOLS the City admits for internal use — the agentic-substrates the Mage's spawning ceremony operates against.

A project can be both a kindred-X relationship and a Bestiary entry. AAIF (Linux Foundation) is the kindred-protocol or kindred-ecosystem behind Goose; Nous Research is a project that the City has not yet formally entered into a kindred-X relationship with but whose Hermes substrate the City admits.

The two registers compose without colliding. The kindred-X register answers *what is the relationship between agentprivacy and the other project*. The Bestiary answers *what tools does the City spawn at the Threshold*.

## Honesty discipline

- **Operational** — every Bestiary entry MUST link to a live, public, open-source project. No fictional substrates. No vapourware.
- **Architectural** — each entry's iconographic-affinity determination is editorial; reasonable disagreement at the staff/companion boundary is documented in the entry, not resolved by fiat.
- **Conjectural** — entries may carry conjecture references (e.g., Hermes carries C56 and C57), but the conjectures' status (architectural / conjectural / held-open) is named explicitly.

The Bestiary is open by design. The Sovereign who recognises a substrate the City should admit may propose the admission as a Tome VI act.
