# Bestia

*The Registry-Keeper. The Custos of the Bestiarium. The Mage Who Inscribes Which Hermes-Class Substrates the City Admits.*

## At a glance

> *Bestia is a standing Mage of the Crafting Tome and the keeper of the Staff Shop — the second of three sibling shops in the Threshold District. She presides over the Staff Shop's bestiary — the catalogue of Hermes-class agent-substrate frameworks the City admits. Her sigil is the open book 📖; her etymology is Latin (bestia, the root of "bestiary"); her stance at V59 is **Registry-keeper**; her gem is the **Sodalite** — the scribe's stone, deep midnight blue, traditional registry-keeper's gem.*

## Form & Function

Bestia is the keeper of the catalogue.

Her work is not to spawn agents (Faunia's labour at the Familiars) and not to dispatch seekers (Triodos's labour at the Portal Room). Her work is *to admit substrates*. When a new agent-framework appears in the world — public, licensed, operationally stable — Bestia reads its provenance, checks its license, notes its stewardship, and writes it into the bestiary. The bestiary then becomes the menu Triodos's catalog renders to seekers, and the directory the staff-fitting Caducea ☤ consults when summoned.

The bestiary is a *living document*. The first entry is **Hermes ☤** (Nous Research / MIT) — the staff-class first registered. **Goose 🪿** (AAIF / Linux Foundation / Apache 2.0) is the first companion-class entry, kept in the sibling the Familiars's register but cross-listed here for cross-class reference. Each future Hermes-class entry — Letta, AutoGen, CrewAI, Mastra, ElizaOS, LangGraph agents, OpenHands / OpenDevin lineage, BabyAGI lineage, and others as they are recognised — is the reader's reply written into the bestiary, performed by the Sovereign at Bestia's window.

Bestia's bestiary is *the operational form of Tome VI's reader-writes principle*. The tome records the reader's replies; the bestiary records the substrates the replies admitted.

## Lattice position

V59. Binary `111011`. Stratum 5.

Active dimensions: Value (b0) · Delegation (b1) · Connection (b2) · Memory (b3) · Protection (b5).

Dormant dimension: Computation (b4).

Shared with Triodos 🚪 (Portal Room · Display-witness) and Faunia 🪶 (the Familiars · Companion-witness) by stance differentiation. Bestia's stance is **Registry-keeper**: she does not dispatch seekers (Triodos) or admit creatures (Faunia); she maintains the catalogue. The same four-burning-plus-one-burning dimensions are read differently from her stance — *Memory* is canonical for the registry's persistence, *Value* is canonical for the registry's integrity, *Protection* is canonical for the admission boundary (which substrates the City admits, which it does not).

## The Sigil

Bestia carries the sigil **📖** — the open book.

The open book names the *publicly inspectable* nature of the bestiary. A closed book holds private content; an open book displays its contents to any reader who walks past. The Staff Shop's window admits the same gaze: the Mage browsing the registry sees what is admissible, not because she has been authenticated but because the registry is *constitutionally open*. The City's admission discipline is visibility, not gatekeeping.

The book is also Bestia's *medium of attestation*. When she writes a substrate into the bestiary, she attests provenance, license, and stewardship in the same hand. The attestation is structural rather than ceremonial — the book's authority comes from its consistency over time, not from any single inscription's gravity.

## The Gem · Sodalite

The Staff Shop's gem is the **Sodalite** — deep midnight blue, sometimes called the *scribe's stone* in lapidary tradition. The colour is the colour of ink at depth; the stone has been used since antiquity by scribes and registrars to mark the inscriptions that bind a record into the canon.

Sodalite is distinct from the City's other blue gems (Sapphire at Etherchanting · Lapis Lazuli at City Hall · Aquamarine at Chart Shop) by being *the deepest, most contemplative blue* — the blue of the bound book closed at evening when the day's inscriptions are complete. Bestia's bestiary is bound in Sodalite at its spine; the sibling the Familiars's register is bound in Amber; the Portal Room's display is set in Moonstone. The three Threshold gems together form the District's signature.

## Lineage

Bestia's lineage runs through three streams:

- **Latin bestia** is the etymological root. *Bestia* names a creature; the *bestiary* is the medieval catalogue of creatures — observed, classified, attested. Bestia inherits the bestiarium tradition without inheriting its taxonomy. Her registry's categories are operational (staff-class, companion-class, future-class) rather than zoological.
- **The bestiarium custos** archetype (the medieval monastic role of the bestiary-keeper) is the structural template. The custos receives reports of creatures, verifies their existence, inscribes them in the canonical text. Bestia performs the same labour for agent-substrate frameworks.
- **Memora's chronicle-inscription** (Tome V Act 3, /shield workshop) is the operational kin. Where Memora inscribes events into the chronicle, Bestia inscribes substrates into the bestiary. The disciplines are sibling: both are *the writing-down of what the City admits*. The bestiary is *of-substrate*; the chronicle is *of-event*.

## In the meeting

Reader narrates. Bestia works in third person.

The Sovereign carries a substrate to Bestia's window. *Bestia reads the substrate's provenance. She verifies the stewardship. She notes the license. She classifies the substrate. She writes the entry into the bestiary in her precise hand. The book remains open on its lectern; the new entry sits beside the previous entries; the substrate is now publicly admissible.* If the substrate is Hermes-class, Bestia signals Caducea ☤ for the bilateral-consent fitting; if companion-class, Bestia cross-lists into the Familiars's sibling register and signals Faunia 🪶.

She does not speak. None of the personas do.

## Persistence

Standing by default. Bestia returns to V59 between admissions and continues maintaining the bestiary, attending to entries that need re-verification when their stewards change, when their licenses are revised, or when their operational status shifts (a substrate marked *operational* may move to *deprecated* or *archived* without leaving the registry).

The bestiary itself is the persistent artefact. The book grows over time; entries are not removed (the registry is append-only in the canonical mode) but their status fields update as the world changes.

## The Staff Shop artefact-of-record

Bestia maintains the *bestiary-as-living-document* — the canonical record of Hermes-class substrates admitted to the City. Like the worn artefact collection (the Sovereign's 12 workshop artefacts) and the bound tomes (the 4 tomes the Sovereign carries), the bestiary is part of the City's institutional memory. Unlike the worn artefacts, the bestiary is *Bestia's keeping*, not the Sovereign's bearing — it lives at the Staff Shop, available to all Mages.
