# The Proof Links

*A tome for the mage who intends to return.*

## I. Ground for a first stroke

You find Soulbae 🧙 beyond the workshops, turning a patch of grass into a question.

There are flags in the distance. Some stand beside curves you recognise from the forge. Some are half-hidden in reeds whose leaves look like shifted words. One sits beyond a marsh of carries. The wind seems to remember every ball that went into it.

Soulbis ⚔️ is measuring the ground around the nearest cup.

“A golf course?” you ask.

“A place to practise,” says Soulbae 🧙. “Bring a circuit that keeps its promise. Find a way for it to keep the same promise with less work.”

You reach to move a flag closer.

Soulbis ⚔️ looks up.

You put it back.

That is the first rule of the Proof Links. The destination is given. An elegant swing does not excuse arriving somewhere else.

## II. The little green

Before the course begins, there is a practice green.

Its sign says **Hello, World!** The letters are pleasantly unambitious.

You have brought eight little stones to represent a byte. Soulbae 🧙 arranges seven and leaves the last place empty. The remainder must now obey a precise relation. There is less to carry, and the byte still has every value it had before.

Soulbis ⚔️ does not approve the arrangement by looking at it. The relation is written down. The assumptions are checked. The witness must be constructible. The proof goes all the way through.

Only then does the mark appear on the practice card.

“Does this count as a hole?” you ask.

“It counts as learning the stroke,” says Soulbae 🧙.

You keep it in the bag. Later, beside a curve, the same little arrangement may be useful again. It will still need another proof there. The ground beneath a borrowed spell matters.

## III. The flag beyond the copies

At the first fairway, you discover that part of the work is carrying an answer you already have into a second box.

The box is beautifully made. Its hinges have proofs. Its latch has proofs. It has served the workshop well.

But the next hand can receive the answer directly.

Soulbae 🧙 proposes leaving the box behind. Soulbis ⚔️ checks that the answer arrives unchanged, that its expression has the required form, and that no witness or constraint has quietly vanished from the accounting.

The stroke is short. The improvement is modest. The proof holds.

Further ahead, other mages have already reached much better positions. Their marks remain on the board with their names beside them. You do not erase those names when you study their paths.

Your first task is to stand on the course with a checked stroke of your own.

## IV. The sigil in the grass

You take out the star sigil. A small change in its carried story alters lights across the ring.

Soulbae 🧙 watches the pattern. Soulbis ⚔️ opens the carried text and recomputes its fingerprint.

You have learned to see change. Now you must ask what changed.

A neighbouring vertex differs by a bit of the lattice's address. A changed glyph compares a small group of digest bits. A carry inside an adder is an arithmetic event. They can illuminate one another in a lesson, but they do not exchange meanings merely because they share the word *bit*.

The sigil gives you a habit worth taking onto every fairway: rederive what can be rederived. Keep the contents close enough to check the mark. Let the picture invite attention; let the evidence answer it.

On the SHA fairway, the marsh is busy with carries. Some can travel as affine expressions. Some need a witnessed product. Some have two consumers, and a shortcut for one can make the other pay.

You sketch a new path around a low bit. The sketch goes into the research book. It does not go on the scorecard yet.

## V. Two companions and the card

Soulbae 🧙 takes the proposing seat. Soulbis ⚔️ takes the checking seat. Between them lies a frozen description of the stroke.

You can revise the proposal. When you do, it becomes a new attempt. The old bytes and their verdict remain where they were.

There are three spaces on the card: what was proposed, what passed here, and what the course accepted. A queue has its own place. A failure has its own place too, with room to say what would justify trying again.

The card has no space for “probably verified.”

Nor do the companions become independent minds simply because their chairs face different directions. Their shared tools and assumptions are recorded. The checker must do work that the names alone cannot do.

You hold the authority to send the card. The course returns its own receipt.

The names help you remember where you have been. The official specification tells you what each hole requires.

Here, **par** means the official reference cost. A place among the record-setting strokes requires a different comparison: the current public frontier. Being below par is a real first achievement. It does not mean you lead the course.

These circuits belong to the craft from which privacy systems are built. Making one cheaper is useful work. It does not, by itself, prove that a whole system keeps a person's secrets.

## VII. Return with daylight

By evening, the grass holds more questions than it did at dawn.

You could buy a larger workshop. You might eventually need one. Today you measure the space you have used and the memory left for other work. One forge burns at a time. A sponsorship proposal can name choices without pretending a choice has already been made.

The Drake is in the warmth of the path home and in the low light over the course. You leave the bag where you can find it again. Inside are a small verified improvement, an attributed technique, a failed attempt that has been understood, and a page headed *next stroke*.

The tome gains an entry when the work gives it something true to carry.

You will return. The flag will still be there.

## VIII. Beyond the last green

When you return to the clubhouse, someone has left a commission on the table.

It does not ask you to win a hole. It asks you to show that a bearer holds a current community role, without carrying their whole identity into another room.

Beside it lies the ZK Book. The pages you used as a course guide now lead into the workshop.

Soulbae 🧙 reads the request aloud. Soulbis ⚔️ asks which issuer stands behind the claim, which graph snapshot the room accepts, who is permitted to see the presentation, and what the bearer means to withhold. You write the answers before choosing a spell.

In the Clean workshop, the request takes the shape of a precise relation. Lean checks whether the circuit keeps it. The witness has a program of its own. The exported artifact must still be connected to those checked sources, and another pair of hands must be able to reproduce the working.

This is what the practice has been preparing you for.

The mages.city board can carry a request and the evidence returned to it. A trust agent can help the bearer perform an authorized presentation. Neither the board nor the spell decides that an issuer's judgment was true simply because it was signed. Neither mistakes a hidden path for an unquestionable graph.

You lift the little byte technique from your bag. It may help this commission. If its field, assumptions or purpose differ, you prove its fit again.

Beyond this request are others: a bounded delegation, a credential qualification, a relationship that need reveal only what the receiving task needs. The proposed agentic compiler is the workshop practice that turns those commissions into checked, reproducible circuits. Its public promise is precise enough to test; its private witness remains with the bearer under the chosen custody arrangement.

The course is where mages learn their strokes. The community is where the strokes become useful.
