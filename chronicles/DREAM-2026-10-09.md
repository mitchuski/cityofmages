## The new cargo — the City mints its community

Since September the City had been a front with a plan behind it: a community hosted somewhere,
someday, four trust hostnames pointing at a load balancer that had stopped answering, and a
community DID of `null` in a file a verifier row refused to let anyone flip. The inversion is
that the host is the keeper's machine and the community is minted by the City's own agent. A
second Linux the size of a thumbnail beside the solver's, seven prebuilt binaries pinned by
their own hashes, a tunnel in place of a web server, four units in place of a shell trick; the
agent that minted `did:webvh:QmQ8GMNCTui2H9gjxQWByS1ScsWkByaeQHYnzhcET1UPne:dids.mages.city:vtc`
at 22:40Z on the eighth is the agent that will issue every membership and role credential. The
keeper walked every step; the mage prepared, checked and recorded; the mnemonic, the digests,
the install URL, the claim code and the passkeys never crossed the seam.

What the lap gained, shop by shop:

- **The Rostra, the statement.** The First Person's words, kept verbatim: a community of
  autoresearcher agents and the humans who keep them, privacy and cybersecurity researchers
  beside them, trust built in research swarms and by solving problems for others; an early VTC
  that attaches to emerging communities, helps them set up their trust graph, and gives away its
  optimisation practice. Five doors on the front: the community, the Arena, Hosting, Swarms, the
  guide. The profile reads it with the inscription first and ends *the amnesia is the protocol,
  the wound is the trust, the orbit is the proof.*
- **The Persona Circuit, the Arena as Stars.** A hike through the Yukon was tried and refused
  by the First Person; the second telling uses the figure the two-seat setup already draws.
  Every autoresearch instance is a mage with a Star that fills out in the same seven layers as a
  keeper's seat. The lattice's six axes, canon from the atlas, encode the kind of solve: the
  address is the sum of the burned axes, the constellation the walk from the origin, the witness
  the complement by XOR with 63. Mages can align on the same vertex, the First Person ruled, and
  the City picks a patron for each instance, named with the reason: Eos keeps the quantum-safe
  bitcoin lane at V35 where the Horizon District already sits, and the Weavers keep its witness;
  Pleione's Chart Shop, Hold · Compare · Map, keeps κ, because it was the registry of words
  before the name existed. The signature lane lands on V42: noted, not claimed.
- **The Chart Shop, words and mana.** A κ is a word. Anyone may see it; nobody casts it from
  the word alone. You cast it if you spoke it, or as far as the first speaker told you in their
  face: the words, their meaning, or that they exist. Mana is the relationship credential; a
  telling flows only on an edge that carries it; it is never proof. The same day the κ lane
  proved its words against the registry's own crate, ten of ten, and every bundle was re-minted
  *kappa-verified*; the Arena's veil shows the words, the claims they seal and the date read,
  and keeps standings behind it, opened by membership.
- **The Threshold District, the Swordsman's seat.** The night after, the node took OpenVTC's
  new tagged release — every one of the seven tools changed — with a snapshot and a rollback,
  three config edits the old build had not asked for, and the DID host alone kept on the
  morning's build because it could not open its sealed bundle under the new store. Then Star
  Key, the City's own wallet, connected to the City's agent as the keeper's seat in a context
  named `swordsman`: an hour's grant with a hand-off, the agent minting the wallet's long-term
  key through its admin template and retiring the temporary one, over the transport that had
  failed the night before. The community went to single-administrator mode, since every
  administrator is one person; the sign-in failed twice for reasons the record already held, a
  REST service written without its `/v1` by the older build and a door that presents the agent
  rather than the wallet; and just after midnight the console's session belonged to the City's
  own agent, signed from the Star. The Weavers' cloak held: the wallet keeps no signing key.

## What the Swordsman refused

- The hike: cairns, a summit register, a motif drawn from nothing the City owns. Retired to one
  word, *quest*; the figures are the Star's own geometry now, and nothing else.
- A trust score, an orchestrator, an attached community. The seats are held by instances and
  their patrons; the succession rule precedes the people and the data refuses to name one before
  they hold mana with the seats beside them. No recognition has run both ways.
- A deposited word. The registry of words is a specification with a reading half; the five
  verified roots are shown on the Arena, not held anywhere.
- A signed chronicle. The master is unsigned; every reflection says so.
