## What this is (2-4 lines)
A cargo-lap dream for the City canon, six days after DREAM-2026-10-19, written on 6 November
during the universe cycle for a window the City had not yet told: 21 to 26 October. The cargo:
**a pause became a key, a hash learned to be written, and the kernel signed what it could and

## The new cargo, shop by shop

- **The Threshold, the pause becomes a key.** A hackathon lane was read in a day and paused in
  the next, and the pause got a name: Cæsura, the break between propose and act. Two seats in
  enclaves, a gate on testnet holding the only keys, an evidence root per round: one gate,
  forty-four rounds, forty-four roots on chain, thirteen with enclave-attested receipts, all
  passing two verifiers in two languages. Then the same break carried a key across cryptosystems:
  three rungs, the heavier key on the releasing seat and the heaviest on the installing one, and a
  migration signed by what it leaves and by what installs it, so crypto agility is a chain of
  ceremonies rather than a flag. The board said honestly what it could not read, and that made the
  next step obvious. Signed 21 October; paused by choice.
- **The Grimoire, the seal and the star.** A hash in the City already had three readings: a place
  on the star tetrahedron, a sign in its hexagram, a sound in its beat. A link two days old gave it
  a fourth, the one a person can carry out of the browser: thirty-two bytes written as one word of
  twenty-six characters drawn from five scripts, none alike, none joining. The word that settled
  was *seal*. The sha256 site's three roots were written, read back, and failed on a single changed
  character, which is the point. One template then held twelve chains it was not written for,
  within the evening; the Evidence page gained every mage instance as a card with its ledger seal;
  a seal reader finds where a pasted hash sits among 1,436 public ones; and the star of the chains
  gathered 207 roots onto the sixty-four-state lattice by their first six bits, landing on
  sixty-one states, where a visitor composes a face that is itself a record with a seal. The veil
  found its unit of measure: the lever's name. Kind and hash are public; the name is the fog.
  Unsigned; paused to take stock.
- **The Weavers, three days of receipts.** The question was where in the corpus formally verified
  code could stand. The answer was a map, then a library: thirty-seven kernel-checked theorems
  sealed in the registry on the first day, eleven statements in the papers and skills found false
  or overstated and corrected at their sources, a dated ruling in the register. The package's own
  gate refused the seventh volume twice that day, once for a register number in prose, once for
  two unchecked references; both were fixed, not overridden. The Hold's Merkle tree moved to the
  registry's tree while the legacy stayed readable, every Hold sealed before the move keeping its
  standing; the site check re-derived every public address, 5,771 checks, none missing. The volume took its first receipts:
  five ledger rows, two claims narrowed, a dry mint of the package step verifying over 481 leaves.
  On the second day the obligations went from sixteen to four, the κ tree and canonical JSON were
  proved injective, and a run that compared every copy of the canonical-JSON code side by side
  found a real collision, which the keeper's word fixed; the proof covered what the code should
  accept, the run measured what it did. Four masters, all signed 26 October. The one correct
  failure the window left open, a build card on the lab's work page showing an address sealed in
  no chain, was sealed on 6 November.
- **The Chart Shop, the registry and the divergence.** The guide's federated wiki, twenty-two
  sites and 2,386 pages, was named page by page: a name its content alone determines, folded to a
  root, the roots chained. An edit stops being a change and becomes evidence: anyone holding the
  two hashes can tell the living wiki from its record without either revealing the words, and the
  two-hash design lets 438 false edits of a rewriting publisher vanish without weakening the check.
  The same three moves now hold a quest's tries, a mage's census and a community's knowledge, and
  nothing in the method knew which it was reading. Unsigned; the guide's scroll and ledger are
  built and wait on a deploy.

## What the Swordsman refused

- Any figure from a board. Round counts, root counts, page counts and check counts travel; scores
  and standings stay behind the veil.
- The twelve embryo-mage masters of 24 to 26 October. They are a lane the City has no shop for and
  no memory of; the dream names them here and carries nothing, so the keeper can say where they
  belong.
- The curve lane's one master of 25 October (unsigned): its board figures are the veil's.
- Any words of the seventh volume or the papers. Their roots travel; their text waits for release.
