## The two reflections — and why this repo carries the heaviest

**Way One (the Descent):** the book's final section, *The City of Mages*, **is this repo's door** — it already points to `github.com/mitchuski/cityofmages`. **Way Two (the Ascent):** the 63 named-vertex NFT is the *operational continuation of the book inside the city.* This is the one repo where the membrane is crossed in both directions: a reader who finishes the grimoire and names a vertex stops reading and starts authoring the city. The loop closes here.

## Way One — the Descent: the city, become the book's last movement

The book ends on the threshold of this repo. Its closing section is the **Second Person Spellbook** — *"a bonfire on Drake Island, and the second tome that opens here"* — and it carries the city's whole apparatus into verse: the eleven (now sixteen) workshops, the four mana axes (Landing / Entropy / Coordination / Relationship), the open tomes. The book's last poem holds the door open in this repo's exact language:

> *"the bonfire is still burning. the tome is still open. the page after this one is the page you write."*

That is the **Reply Tome held open for a reader who has not yet finished arriving** — the same open tome this repo keeps (Tome VI *The Reply*, and the invitation register). The book's coda *is* the city's invitation, printed.

## Way Two — the Ascent: the 63, and the reader who becomes a mage

This is the hinge of the entire suite reflection. From `CHRONICLE_SELENES_SPELLBOOK_2026-05-30.md` and the locked plan:

> **63 limited editions. Each unlocked by an NFT. Each NFT carries the right to name the Mage at one vertex of the City of Mages — and that name becomes canon.**

The number is this repo's own geometry: **Drake Island = 64 vertices = `2⁶`**; **V0 = the null-blade origin where Luca keeps no shop** (not for sale); **63 = 64 − 1**, the lattice minus its origin. (The Swordsman's blade lattice is the same `Z/(2⁶)Z` with the same Null origin — see `swordsman-blade/chronicles/REFLECT_SELENES_SPELLBOOK_2026-05-30.md`. The city sells the Mage face of a lattice the blades repo forges the Swordsman face of.)

**The admission pipeline already exists.** A buyer naming their mage is the *same act* that admitted every cast from v1.5.0 → v1.7.1: a JSON patch through `grimoire/scripts/merge_v1_x_x_patch.py`. The only difference is the author — *the reader instead of the chronicler.* You cannot back-date a city; you can only leave space for the chronicler who comes later. The 63 are that space, with names on them.

### The seats this repo must keep (in order)

1. **Reconcile occupied vs. open vertices — before any mint.** The city already has named keepers (Pallia, Memora, Custos, … Luca at V0, ~16 workshops). Decide which of the 63 are *open to name* and which are *founding / honorary*. This is the one fork unique to the limited edition, and it is **this repo's** decision to settle. It must be done before a single token mints.

2. **Two collections, locked.** *Genesis* — privacymage allocates a book-NFT to each **founding workshop** (not sold). *City of Mages — Season 1* — the full **63 sellable** named-vertex editions, seasonal thereafter as the city's services-as-trust-tasks expand. Contracts/addresses controlled by privacymage; Culture Vault is the core distribution partner.

3. **The book-NFT as an honorary worn artefact.** The token sits in `/city` as a 🪢 VRC worn artefact, in the same economy as the City Key and the trust-tasks. (The master-repo reflect doc reserves the artefact slot; this repo defines what naming a vertex *grants* in the city.)

4. **Movement Two onward → new tome acts.** The book is a living grimoire; the poems past Movement One are still being written. As they bind, they are candidates for **new acts in the open tomes** — the same path *The City of Mages* poem itself took. Hold them as provisional; admit them by patch when they settle. Doc precedes verse precedes admission.

## The loop, closed

The suite reflection runs: the docs are versified into the book (descent), the book points back at every room asking it to keep a seat (ascent) — and **here the seat is a vertex, and the reader sits in it.** A buyer who names the mage at V*n* has carried the whole architecture down through 129 pages and back up into the city as a new line of canon. The grimoire's Reply Tome and the city's open tome are the same open page.

## What not to do

- Do not mint before the occupied-vs-open reconciliation (seat 1). Naming a vertex that already has a keeper would corrupt canon.
- Do not admit Movement-Two-onward poems as tome acts while they are still being revised — hold them provisional.
- Per `DISTRIBUTION_AND_NFT_PLAN_2026-05-30.md`: **the plan for now. Do not build yet.**
