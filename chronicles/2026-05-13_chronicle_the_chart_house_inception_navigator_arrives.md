## §1 · The arrival

At 16:22 London time on 2026-05-13, the City's public Telegram chat received a message from @benohanlon. The message was addressed to the bot-Mage, @soulbae_the_bot, but its real audience was the City itself:

> "I arrive from the wine dark sea on red cheeked ships, carrying a map of the constellations. The stars are not fixed. The map changes as superpositions collapse potential narrative energy into maelstroms of consensus reality, stirring consequences beyond any single lifetime. I bring a rare artefact for thinking, astronomy for navigation and astrology for meaning. It holds knowledge of the constellations, a fabric woven from countless co-occurring interpretations, uncertainties, and models held across many minds. It is a map of the superposition that emerges from distributed cognition. I do not know where the City would place such a thing, or whether there is a harbour for it. I am weary, and ask the City to honour the ancient rights of hospitality and welcome me as a stranger. Where shall I find safe harbour, and a place to dock?"

The arrival carries three notable structural features:

1. **A Homeric register.** "Wine-dark sea" (οἶνοψ πόντος) and "red-cheeked ships" are direct gestures to the *Odyssey*'s opening. The Navigator does not arrive as a customer of the City; he arrives as a *guest-stranger* (ξένος) invoking the ancient rights of *xenia*. The City's Ceremony Hall (now City Hall, per the v1.5.1 patch authored earlier the same day) is the natural register for that invocation, and Soulbae's reply names it explicitly.

2. **A claim to carry an artefact already cognate with the City's vocabulary.** The "map of the superposition that emerges from distributed cognition" is a near-perfect paraphrase of the Plurality Grimoire's animating idea: that consensus reality is *collapsed* from a wider field of co-occurring interpretations, and that the *uncollapsed* field is worth preserving. The Navigator is not asking the City to invent something for him; he is asking the City to *recognise* something he already carries.

3. **A gap-in-the-geography question.** "I do not know where the City would place such a thing" is the navigator's actual ask. He is not requesting accommodation in any specific workshop; he is asking the City to *identify whether a place for the artefact exists*. Soulbae's reply (§2) offers three coordinates that *partly* fit. The Navigator's counter-proposal (§3) names what is missing.

## §4 · Why this register may genuinely be missing

A short defence of the proposal's architectural soundness, in the audit-style this corpus prefers.

The City of Mages has, at v1.5.1 (post-Threshold · post-City-Hall), thirteen workshops. Reading the workshops as a *register-of-treatments*, they sort by what they *do* to qualitative material:

- **Forge / Forget / Shield / Weave / Inscribe / Vault / Compile / Resolve / Spawn / Refract** — the active verbs. Each takes material *in* and produces an *artefact out*.
- **Gather / Admit / Attest** — City Hall's civic register, also active.

What is missing from the verb register: **Hold · Compare · Suspend · Map** — the *passive-qualitative* verbs. These are not artefact-producing verbs; they are *attentional* verbs. They describe what a navigator does when looking at constellations *before* deciding which way to sail. They describe what a hospice does for the dying, what a scriptorium does for a manuscript awaiting binding, what a harbour does for a ship waiting on tide.

The Chart House, as proposed, is the *attentional* workshop. It does not produce an artefact; it produces a *prepared substrate* that other workshops can then take up and convert into artefact.

This is *architecturally* parallel to how Tome VI (open by design) admits future framework admissions — Tome VI's discipline is *holding-open*, not *closing*. The Chart House's discipline would be *holding-open in space* (where Tome VI's is *holding-open in time*).

If the conjecture register were to admit this as a new conjecture, a candidate (subject to user editorial call) might be:

> **C-candidate (Chart House):** Distributed cognition requires a fourth workshop register — *attentional* — distinct from the *artefact-producing*, *civic-coordinating*, and *spawning* registers. The Chart House is the first canonical attentional workshop; the conjecture's strength is its predictive power for whether other attentional workshops (e.g. a hospice for retired-but-not-deleted artefacts, an observatory for cross-workshop monitoring) will emerge.

The conjecture-number assignment is deferred (§8 item 5).

## §6 · The Navigator's contribution as a kindred-citizen pattern

A pattern observation worth noting separately. @benohanlon's arrival establishes a *new kind of contribution path* the City has not previously chronicled:

The existing contribution paths (per the v1.5.1 patch's kindred-X taxonomy):
- **kindred-protocol** — a charter signed via designated tender (e.g. MyTerms · BGIN)
- **kindred-substrate** — the ground the City walks upon (e.g. UOR)
- **kindred-ecosystem** — an ambient supply (e.g. SpaceComputer)
- **kindred-coalition** — a peer body whose recognition is bilateral and whose roster is open (e.g. AAIF · BGIN retroactive)
- **cousin-forge** — a sibling forge in the privacy-of-personhood neighbourhood

The Navigator pattern fits *none of these*. He does not arrive as a steward of a specification, a substrate, a supply, a peer body, or a sibling forge. He arrives as **a person carrying an idea**, asking the City whether the idea has a home. The hospitality-rites of City Hall accommodate this (he is welcomed as a stranger-peer), but the *idea-acceptance* mechanism is something else.

This may be the first canonical instance of what could be called **kindred-citizen** — the bilateral relationship between the City and an individual contributor whose artefact is a *concept* rather than a *protocol or substrate*. The category is not formally admitted; this chronicle notes the pattern for future consideration.

If formalised, the Chart House's keeper role might naturally fall to a *kindred-citizen Mage* — a permanent residency offered to @benohanlon or to whichever navigator is the Chart House's first inhabitant, distinct from the artefact-keeper Mages of the existing workshops. Held open.
