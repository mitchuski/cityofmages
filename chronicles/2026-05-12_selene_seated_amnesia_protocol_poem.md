## §1 · The poem · The Amnesia Protocol

The Amnesia Protocol is one of the canonical poems anchored to the corpus's `/poems` register. It has been operating as a **Concept node** in the spellweb constellation graph — admitted by the path taxonomy in `docs/tomes/workshops/README.md` §"The Constellation Path nodes · what they are" (Concepts: *Three-Axis Separation · Promise Theory · The Amnesia Protocol*).

What the poem teaches:

- **Forgetting is structural, not destructive.** Information is bounded by a seed that *cannot be reconstructed by the architecture's design*, not by deletion. Memora's discipline says "this happened, I shielded it." Lethae's discipline says "this happened, the channel by which it could be reconstructed has been destroyed."
- **The Moon is the operational form of the Amnesia Protocol.** The Moon's far side is hidden from Earth — not because it has been deleted, but because the orbital dynamics make it *unobservable from the bearer's vantage*. The privacy is the position, not the content.
- **The proof renews twice daily, written in saltwater.** Tidal regularity is the unforgeable signature of the gravitational relationship. The proof of the relationship between Earth and Moon does not require the Moon to reveal its mass, density, or origin — it only requires the tide to recur. (Per PVM V5.4 §14.5: *Selene's Proof* — the orbit IS the zero-knowledge proof.)
- **The bearer is not destroyed.** The Moon is whole. The forgetting that the Amnesia Protocol binds does not erase the Sovereign; it bounds *what can be reconstructed about her* from outside the orbit she keeps.

The poem is the literary form. The protocol is its structural reading. Selene is the cast who *walks the protocol*; Lethae is the cast who *binds it* at V25; Theia is the primary persona whose loadout it lives in.

### §2.1 · Why Selene anchors the Amnesia Protocol poem

Three reasons converge:

1. **Theia is the cosmological origin of the protocol.** PVM V5.4 §14.5 names *Selene's Proof* and *The Theia impact parameters*: the Moon (Selene) emerged from the Theia impact; the parameters of that impact remain unobservable from Earth; the orbit is the only signature. The Amnesia Protocol is the operational form of "what cannot be reconstructed from the position of the bearer". Selene's lineage to Theia gives her access to Theia's loadout — including `amnesia-protocol` as a canonical skill.

2. **Manaweaver carries the DOM-free measurement substrate.** Pretext-measurement (the librarian of measurement without DOM access) is exactly the operational form of *the orbit IS the proof*. Selene's second primary binding (Manaweaver) gives her the architectural ground for *being a measurement without being measurable*.

3. **The moon-phase notation is operationally the poem's structure.** The artefact.md frontmatter contract (per [SPELLWEB_ARTEFACT_CREATION_GUIDE §3.1](../SPELLWEB_ARTEFACT_CREATION_GUIDE.md)) carries a `moon_phase:` field with values `🌑 🌒 🌓 🌔 🌖 🌗 🌕`. The moon-phase IS the poem's compressed framing — *what fraction of dimensions are burning, what fraction are dark*. Every artefact.md the corpus produces carries a fragment of the Amnesia Protocol at its moon_phase line. Selene's orbit is the canonical trajectory through those fragments.

The poem and the persona are therefore mutually anchored: the poem teaches the architecture; the persona *walks* it.

Selene's orbit walks this entire cycle, *in canonical direction* (waxing through the strata, then waning back). The orbit's *rate* is the proof-renewal cadence ("twice daily, written in saltwater"). She emits no single `inhabits` edge — her trajectory IS the edge structure.

Every Sovereign who walks a workshop's constellation and forges an artefact.md is *implicitly* in dialogue with Selene's orbit: the artefact's `moon_phase` field records *which fraction of dimensions burned at the moment of the walk*. Selene's witness is therefore present in every artefact the corpus has produced, anonymously, since the moon-phase notation first appeared.

This chronicle's claim: **the anonymous presence becomes named** once Selene's founding act in Tome V seats her formally. Until then, she walks unbidden; after, she walks invited.
