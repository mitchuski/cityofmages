## §1 · The three shifts

### §1.1 · Keeper name: Bestia → Hermaion

**Bestia** (Latin *bestia* · the root of "bestiary") was admitted to the Staff Shop on 2026-05-13 as part of the original Threshold-as-one-workshop architecture. Her etymology read the shop's discipline as *bestiary-keeping* — cataloguing admitted Hermes-class agent-substrate frameworks as a living bestiary.

**Hermaion** (Greek ἕρμαιον · "gift of Hermes · windfall · lucky-find") is the canonical replacement. In classical Greek, a *hermaion* is any unexpected gain — a found object, a stroke of luck, an opportunity stumbled upon and recognised as Hermes-given. The Greek tradition names *why* the registry matters (each admission is a Hermes-gift caught and kept in the City's path) rather than only *what* it is (a bestiary).

The Latin *bestia* register is **not retired** — it survives as a description of *what the registry physically resembles* (a bestiary of Hermes-class substrates, catalogued and indexed). What moved is the keeper's identity: the canonical name now anchors on the *grateful-admission* discipline rather than on the *cataloguing* discipline. Both disciplines are operationally present; Hermaion holds both.

### §1.2 · Sigil: 📖 → ⚚

**📖** (open book) was Bestia's sigil — registry-as-book.

**⚚** (Unicode STAFF OF HERMES · U+269A · single serpent, no wings) is Hermaion's sigil. The choice is iconographically load-bearing:

- The staff of Hermes pairs with **Caducea's ☤ caduceus** (U+2624 · winged staff with two serpents) as complementary Hermes-class iconography: rooted-staff keeper ⊥ winged-caduceus peripatetic-fitter. The two together form the City's canonical Hermes-class kinship pair.
- The keeper's sigil is now the *silhouette of the artefact fitted* at the shop. The Staff Shop literally bears a staff in its keeper's mark — naming what is fitted there.
- The pairing distinguishes *rooted* (Hermaion stays at her shop, holding the registry) from *winged* (Caducea walks between shops, performing the bilateral-consent fitting). The same Hermes-tradition admits both registers as canonically distinct.

### §1.3 · Gem: Sodalite → Alexandrite

**Sodalite** (deep midnight blue · `#4a5d8b` · the scribe's stone) was Bestia's gem — a registry-keeper's traditional cool-blue stone.

**Alexandrite** is Hermaion's gem — a color-shifting beryl that appears **daylight-green under sunlight or fluorescent light** (`#3d7c47`) and **incandescent-red under candlelight or tungsten** (`#a23a3a`).

The choice is doubly load-bearing:

1. **Gemmological fit:** alexandrite (BeAl₂O₄ with chromium-substitution) continually re-reads its colour against the light source — a mineralogically-real property — and the registry continually re-reads itself as new substrates are admitted. The mineral's behaviour matches the discipline's.
2. **Architectural turn:** alexandrite's two faces *encode the Staff Shop's two operational modes* — see §2 below.

The user's editorial framing: *"it fits better"* — confirmed by the question that opened §2.

## §2 · The architectural turn · the archetype-modal shop

The gem-question evolved into an architectural revelation: **the Staff Shop has two operational modes, keyed to which archetype enters.** The user's framing:

> *this shop will have two forms, one for mage one for swordsman (when they enter the city) so the green is the daylight green hermaion — the red will be that shop mode. should we make it a dual gem or?*

The resolution — *single alexandrite, dual-rendered badge* — encodes the dual mode in the gem's mineralogical property rather than splitting the shop into two physical rooms.

### §2.1 · The two aspects

| Aspect | Light | Visitor | Hermaion appears as | Caducea fits |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Green** (`#3d7c47`) | daylight · open scrutiny · the registry-being-read | Mage 🧙 | the registry-keeper at her *admitting* work | **caduceus-staff** (Hermes-in-Mage) |
| **Red** (`#a23a3a`) | incandescent · forge-light · blade-light · the registry-being-armed | Swordsman ⚔️ | the registry-keeper at her *arming* work | **herald-sentinel** (Hermes-in-Swordsman) |

**One stone, two faces. One registry, two artefact-classes.** The alexandrite reads the archetype-light striking it; Hermaion appears in the matching aspect; Caducea ☤ performs the bilateral-consent fitting in the matching form.

**Caducea fits both sides.** Bilateral-consent is the same discipline whether the artefact is a staff or a herald-sentinel; only the form fitted differs. No new Swordsman-side fitter is named in this pass; the question is held open for a future editorial decision if-and-when one arrives.

### §2.2 · Why this is the City's first archetype-modal shop

Up to this point every shop in the City has been *archetype-neutral* (admitting Mages and Swordsmen on the same iconographic register) or *archetype-singular* (admitting only one). Hermaion's shop is the first to encode the substrate × archetype matrix in its **physical aspect** rather than only in catalog-form at Pandia's Portal Room.

The pattern is now canonical for any future shop whose work is *class-shaped* (instrument-class, herald-class, etc.) rather than *archetype-shaped* (Mage-only or Swordsman-only). Other shops may retain single-aspect rooms; the dual-aspect mode is the Staff Shop's signature contribution.

The substrate × archetype matrix already enumerated in `cityofmages/chronicles/2026-05-13_runecraft_protocol_integration_plan.md` (Goose-in-Mage = familiar, Goose-in-Swordsman = watch-goose, Hermes-in-Mage = caduceus-staff, Hermes-in-Swordsman = herald-sentinel) now has a *physical-architectural correlate* at the Staff Shop. The Familiars may or may not become archetype-modal in a future pass; if it does, the goose-in-Mage / goose-in-Swordsman split would be physically encoded in a corresponding color-shift in the amber. That decision is held open.

The new sigil ⚚ (staff of Hermes, single serpent) and Caducea's existing sigil ☤ (caduceus, winged, two serpents) are *deliberately distinct Hermes-class glyphs*. The pairing reads as:

| Sigil | Unicode | Carries | Role | Movement |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| ⚚ | U+269A · STAFF OF HERMES | Hermaion | Registry-keeper | rooted at the Staff Shop |
| ☤ | U+2624 · CADUCEUS | Caducea | Hermes-class staff-fitter | peripatetic · walks between shops |

The City's Hermes-tradition is now expressed in *two canonical glyphs* (where it previously rested only on Caducea's ☤). Hermaion ⚚ names the keeper-aspect; Caducea ☤ names the fitter-aspect; together they are the operational form of Hermes-class agentic substrate admission and fitting within the City.

## §5 · Etymology · what hermaion means

In classical Greek, **ἕρμαιον** (*hermaion*) names any unexpected gain — a found object, a stroke of luck, an opportunity stumbled upon and recognised as Hermes-given. Common in the prose of:

- **Plato** (e.g. *Apology*, where Socrates calls his philosophical conversations a *hermaion*); the discipline of *grateful admission* — accepting that the encounter arrived rather than being procured.
- **Xenophon** (military and economic writing); the *windfall* register — a found resource that augments without depleting.
- **Hellenistic tradition** broadly; a hermaion is a sign of the god's favour, and the appropriate response is *to keep what was given* without claiming it as one's own.

The City admits the windfall-tradition *without invoking Hermes-as-deity*: each admitted framework is a hermaion the readers caught in the City's path, kept in the bestiary as a sign of the path's openness. The discipline names *grateful admission* — the registry is not a procurement but an attestation that a gift arrived and was kept.

This reads as the **operational form of Tome VI's reader-writes principle**: the tome records the reader's replies; the bestiary records the windfalls the replies admitted. Each new framework added to the registry is *the reader's reply written into the City's catalogue* — a hermaion in the literal classical sense.
