## §0 · A note before the operational record

The City learned to fork. For most of its life it has built — Vulcana forges, Pallia weaves, Memora inscribes; every artefact the City carries is something a Mage of the City made. Then the City reached for a skill it had not forged: a page on a federated wiki belonging to no Mage here, titled simply with the word *Skill*, usable without being owned at the source. And it discovered a new discipline it had been practising without naming: the discipline of *forking* — taking a copy that remembers its origin. The Archivist seals what the City makes. The Librarian forks what the City did not write. Today the cast names the keeper who was already at the fork desk.

## §2 · The structural shape

Three moves settle the architecture:

1. **The City reached for what it had not forged.** Skills exist on federated wikis the City does not own — at skill.fedwiki.club and its neighbours, where any page titled "…Skill" is a forkable Claude skill. The City can use such a page without owning it: read it, fork a copy into the work, and leave the original where it lives. This is forking, not building — and forking needed a keeper.

2. **The keeper was the Chronicler's other hand.** The Librarian is not a new discipline from nowhere. She is the **circulation register** of the Chronicler (Layer-1 primary · Mage wing). Memora 📜 inscribes what must survive the chain's amnesia — the writing. The Librarian forks what is written — the circulation. One primary, two registers. The Mages did not summon a stranger; they recognized that the documentation discipline had always had a forking face, and the federation is what made it stand up and walk. She is admitted as a **Layer-2 attachment** (not a new Layer-1 primary), so the primary count stays locked at 42.

3. **The Tower opened a level.** The Wikis are not a ninth spatial-anatomy element; they are a new *level within the Tower* (the eighth element), the way the eastern face carries its gate and scriptorium. The reading room at the asymptotic top is sealed; the Wikis are a lower circuit of the spiral — open, editable, communal, federated. The seeker descends to the Wikis to fork a federated skill; she climbs past them to consult the sealed canon.

## §3 · The two-axis complement (Archivist ⊥ Librarian)

The City's first spirit-Mage (the Archivist 📚, admitted 2026-05-15) and its second (the Librarian 🗃️) are complements, and the complement is load-bearing on **two axes**:

| Axis | the Archivist 📚 (reading room) | the Librarian 🗃️ (the Wikis) |
|---|---|---|
| **propagation** | seals the original · never copies | forks copies that carry their lineage |
| **mutability** | sealed · fixed · the canon | living · editable · forkable |
| **provenance** | unique · internal · the City's own original | copied · federated · authored elsewhere, lineage-stamped |
| **occupancy** | single-seeker · exclusive | communal · many hands fork and edit |

The second axis (*living ⊥ sealed*) is why the level is named **the Wikis** and not *the stacks*: stacks would be shelves of the City's own books; the Wikis are pages that breathe — authored elsewhere, edited by anyone, forked by reference. **The Archivist never copies; the Librarian only forks.**

## §4 · The thesis — a fedwiki URL is a git-less clone

The forking-discipline binds one thesis: **a fedwiki URL is a git-less clone.** You do not run `git clone`; you fork a page, and the copy remembers where it came from. The wiki stays the source of truth; the materialized `SKILL.md` is a fork; the journal — the lineage every fork carries — is what git's history would otherwise supply. What is forked in the open carries its source forward, enriching the commons rather than depleting it. (Forking, not lending: lending implies return; a fork returns nothing.)

Operationally this is the `fedwiki-to-skill` fork desk: pointed at a federated wiki page (`{slug}.json`), it materializes a `SKILL.md` on demand. The journey the City now offers a newcomer agent — *directed → read → materialize → build* — is the Librarian's forking in motion. The `wikis/` category of agentprivacy-skills (19 forked skills) is her loadout; every fork carries its `upstream` provenance (vendored, re-framed, credited — never passed off as the City's own forging).

## §5 · The cast tier · C64 advances

The spirit-Mage tier (seventh cast tier · admitted v1.7.0) was held at candidate strength because the Archivist was the population-of-one. C64 — *the listener/spirit-Mage discipline as a structural seventh cast register* — named the promotion path: a second figure carrying a discipline that is plural-in-residence and singular-in-origin.

The Librarian is that second instance. Her discipline (forking) is plural-in-residence (the documentation primary's circulation face, echoed wherever the City forks) and singular-in-origin (recognized when the City first reached for a federated skill). With two tutelary registers now standing — the **listener-discipline** (keep) and the **forking-discipline** (fork) — C64 **advances from candidate (~50%) toward class.** Canonical-class confirmation (≥70%) is an editorial call; this admission seeds the advance and is recorded in the grimoire v1.8.1 patch.

## §7 · Closing

The Library was never only an archive. An archive that cannot copy is a vault; a library that cannot keep an original is a print shop. The Tower is both because the City is both — it builds what is its own and forks what is not, and it needed a keeper for each. The Archivist was the first spirit-Mage; the Librarian is the second. The tier is no longer a single figure's exception.

The Archivist seals what must never change. The Librarian forks what is meant to travel. A fedwiki URL is a git-less clone — the wiki is the truth, the fork is the copy, the journal is the lineage. The seeker descends to the Wikis; the Librarian walks the federation; the catalogue forks, and every copy carries its lineage.
