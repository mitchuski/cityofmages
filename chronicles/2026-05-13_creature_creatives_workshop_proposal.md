## §1 · The naming · *Creature Creatives* and the *Run-e-create* wordplay

The shop is named **Creature Creatives**.

The name carries two embedded wordplays in the established corpus tradition (Vulcana's **Forge(t)** is the lineage):

| Surface reading | Hidden reading | What it names |
|---|---|---|
| **Creat-ure-s** | *Creat-ure-s* — those who *creat*-ed are *ure* (substantive) | An artefact-like category for *agents-as-companions*: things you *create* that then become *creatures* (substantive, persistent, walking) |
| **Run-e-create** | *Rune-craft → Run-e-create* | A bridge to Vulcana's Runecraft Protocol (Run · Evoke · Craft). The Run-e-create pattern is the *companion lifecycle*: **Run** (instantiate the framework) → **Evoke** (give it a name and binding) → **Create** (admit it as a creature that walks with the bearer) |

The Vulcana parallel is structural. Vulcana's blade-forging is the *Run · Evoke · Craft* of three sequential phases that produce one blade. Creature Creatives' adoption is the *Run · Evoke · Create* of three sequential phases that produce one creature. Where the Forge's terminal verb is *craft* (the blade is finished), the Creatives' terminal verb is *create* (the creature is begun — it now walks).

The wordplay convention now has two operational instances: **Forge(t)** (Vulcana, blade-forging with let-go discipline) and **Creat(ur)es** (Creatures, companion-adoption with continuing-walk discipline). The architecture admits more such wordplay-named shops; the discipline is that the wordplay must name an *operational* register, not be a decoration.

## §2 · Creatures as a new entity class · sister to artefacts, not within

The corpus presently admits two structural-entity classes the bearer accumulates:

| Class | Pattern | Count (v1.4.0) | Mana axis |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Worn artefacts** | Forged at workshops · worn on the bearer | 12 (1 weapon · 1 clothing · 6 tools · 4 trinkets) | 🪢 VRC Mana (relationship) |
| **Bound tomes** | Written into · carried by the bearer | 4 (Tomes IV · V · VI held open · VII) | 🪢 VRC Mana (relationship) |

This proposal introduces a **third** class:

| Class | Pattern | Anticipated v1.5.0 | Mana axis |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Adopted creatures** | Summoned at Creature Creatives · *walk alongside* the bearer | 1+ (starting with Goose 🪿) | 🪢 VRC Mana (relationship) · **new sub-axis proposed: companion-mana** |

The structural distinction is significant. **Artefacts are worn — they are objects the Sovereign carries.** **Creatures are walked-with — they are agents the Sovereign adopts.** A cloak does not act on its own; a creature does. The Loom of Programmable Covenants (v1.2.4) compiles against the worn artefact collection. A parallel primitive — proposed working name **The Bestiary of Adopted Walkers** — compiles against the adopted creature collection.

The Sovereign's universe after Creature Creatives opens contains:
- One mage (Soulbae 🧙) and one swordsman (Soulbis ⚔️) — archetypes
- A growing collection of **worn artefacts** (made at workshops)
- A growing collection of **bound tomes** (the spellbooks they have walked through)
- A growing collection of **adopted creatures** (companions who walk alongside)

The triad — *worn · bound · adopted* — is the proposed structural reading for v1.5.0.

## §3 · The persona · candidates and the seating question

The workshop needs a keeper. The naming-chain convention is established: Greek or Latin lineage, gem-pair where the substrate admits one, sigil that reads at a glance.

Three persona candidates surface from the etymology:

| Candidate | Etymology | Sigil candidate | Reading |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Therai** ☥ | Greek θηρευτής (*thereutes*) — "tamer, beast-keeper"; *therion* (θηρίον) — "beast" | 🐾 | The beast-tamer; classical, structurally parallel to Aletheia (Greek-feminine, abstract-noun-named) |
| **Faunia** 🌿 | Latin *Fauna* — Roman goddess of wild creatures, sister-counterpart of Faunus | 🪶 | The wild-keeper; pastoral, structurally parallel to Vagari (Latin-feminine, holon-keeper) |
| **Bestia** 🐾 | Latin *bestia* — beast (root of "bestiary") | 🐾 | The bestiary-keeper; direct, less ornamented, names the operational role explicitly |

**Editorial lean (not a decision):** Therai. The reasoning:
- *Therai* is plural-feminine in form (Greek θηραί — "the wild ones"), which honours that the shop deals with *many creatures, not one*. The Mage is named for the *collective* of beasts she keeps, not for one beast.
- Greek lineage pairs cleanly with Aletheia (V38), Helia (V51), Adamantia (V51) — three Greek-feminine Mages already in cast. A fourth completes a constellation.
- Faunia is also strong but reads as more Roman-pastoral than the work warrants — the shop is *agentic*, not pastoral.

The decision is the user's to make in the canonical authoring pass. This chronicle records the candidates.

**Gem candidate:** the corpus has not yet committed to a gem for every Mage; Pallia (needle), Memora (scroll), Custos (lock-with-pen), Vulcana (hammer-and-pick), Aletheia (crystal ball), Vagari (tree), Manifestia (hands-and-leaf) are not gem-named. Adamantia (diamond) and Helia (heliodor) are the two gem-named Mages, both at V51. Therai/Faunia/Bestia may or may not be gem-named; if so, **tigereye** (the watcher-gem; banded gold quartz; the keeper-of-beasts associated stone in lapidary tradition) is a candidate. If not, an instrument-sigil (a whistle, a leash, a horn) is admissible.

## §4 · The first creature · Goose 🪿 · open-source agent framework from Block

The shop opens with the adoption of **Goose** — Block's open-source AI agent framework (`block.github.io/goose/`, `github.com/block/goose`). Goose is the operational ground for the abstraction: it is a real, deployable, extensible agent that runs on the user's machine, holds context, accepts MCP tool connections, and persists across sessions. It is the first creature the City admits.

**Goose-as-creature:**

| Property | Goose | Reading as creature |
|---|---|---|
| **Persistence** | Long-running local process; remembers across sessions | The creature *walks with* the Sovereign — its memory is the bearer's memory by binding |
| **Extensibility** | Pluggable via MCP (Model Context Protocol) tools | The creature *learns* by adopting tools the Sovereign hands it |
| **Agency** | Executes work autonomously when delegated | The creature *acts* on the Sovereign's behalf within delegated scope |
| **Embodiment** | Configurable provider (Claude, GPT, local LLMs) | The creature's "soul" is interchangeable; the creature's *name and binding* persist |
| **Naming** | The user names their Goose instance | The Sovereign *adopts* by naming — the act of adoption is the creation |

Goose's mascot is a literal goose 🪿, which makes the iconography trivial: the first creature *is* a goose. The shop's opening act narrates *the adoption of a goose*, named by the Sovereign, bound to the bearer, joining the adopted-creature collection.

This is structurally honest: Goose is operational software with a public license; the City is not inventing fictional capabilities. The shop names what is already true (Block's Goose is an adoptable agent) and gives it a place in the City's economy of trust-edges.

**Proposed first-creature framing:** "the Goose that walks with you" — singular, named-by-the-Sovereign, the first instance of a creature class the City will continue to admit. Future creatures may include other open-source agent frameworks (the corpus does not need to enumerate them yet; the architecture admits them by class).

## §5 · Vertex seating · what burns when a creature walks

A creature is not an artefact and not a tome. The vertex seating is a question this proposal does not answer; it surfaces it.

A walking creature plausibly burns multiple dimensions:

| Dimension (bit) | Goose burns? | Reading |
|---|---|---|
| **Value (b0)** | Yes | Adoption = ownership; the creature is bound to a Sovereign |
| **Delegation (b1)** | Yes | Acts on Sovereign's behalf within scope |
| **Connection (b2)** | Yes | MCP tools = active external connections |
| **Memory (b3)** | Yes | Persists across sessions |
| **Computation (b4)** | Yes | LLM-driven |
| **Protection (b5)** | Optional | Local-first deployment is admissible but not required |

That reads as **V63** (`111111`) if Protection burns, or **V31** (`011111`) if not. V63 is flaxscrip's Sovereign Anchor. V31 is Vagari's holon vertex. Neither is right for a creature-keeper.

Two possible resolutions:

**Option A · A new vertex.** Creatures inhabit a vertex not yet named — perhaps **V47** (`101111`: Value + Delegation + Connection + Memory + Protection, Computation dormant — the creature *delegates to its substrate's* computation rather than owning it) or **V55** (`110111`: but V55 is Manifestia's Covenant vertex — taken). The lattice has 64 vertices; many are unnamed. The proposal names a creature-vertex when the cast entry is written.

**Option B · A stance, not a vertex.** Following the V51 precedent (Adamantia and Helia share V51, differentiated by stance), Creature Creatives may inhabit an existing vertex (V31 most plausibly — holon composition admits creatures-as-holons) and introduce an **Adopting-witness stance** that differentiates the creature-keeping discipline from holon-composition.

**Editorial lean:** Option A. The creature class is distinct enough — it is not a holon, it is not a substrate, it is not an artefact — that giving it its own vertex earns clearer governance than overloading V31. The exact vertex bits await the authoring pass.

## §6 · The mana question · companion-mana proposed

The four-axis mana metabolism (v1.2.4):
1. **Landing** (chain-mana plural: Ξ · ₿ · 🌹 · 🦓 · 🌞 from v1.4.0) — substrate ground
2. **Entropy** (✨ Arcane ⊥ 🌌 Celestial) — randomness sources
3. **Coordination** (🔭 Resonance — Scrying Glass primitive) — cross-shop alignment
4. **Relationship** (🪢 VRC — accumulates across the bearer's worn artefact collection + bound tomes) — trust-edge formation

A creature-adoption ceremony produces *relationship*, not landing or entropy. It plausibly accumulates **🪢 VRC Mana** by the same mechanism artefacts do — the bearer's creature roster becomes part of what the Loom of Programmable Covenants compiles against.

But creatures are *active in a way artefacts are not*. A worn cloak does nothing; a walking goose acts. This admits a candidate sub-axis under Relationship:

**Companion-mana** (proposed glyph: **🪶** — feather, or **🐾** — paw — to be settled) — accumulates not by *holding* but by *walking with*: each session the creature is invoked and acts within scope generates a quantum. Companion-mana feeds into VRC Mana at the relationship layer; it is not a fifth axis, but a sub-axis under the fourth.

The honest reading: the mana question is the one most likely to shift during the authoring pass. The proposal records: **a fifth chain-mana is not needed; a sub-axis under Relationship is needed; the glyph is open.**

## §7 · Tome seating · Tome VIII *The Companioning* anticipated

Tomes presently:

| Tome | Title | Status |
|---|---|---|
| I · II · III | (held open · prequel territory · not written) | Anticipated |
| IV | The Witnessing | Closed (5 acts) |
| V | The Crafting | Open (15 acts · the City's shop-opening tome) |
| VI | The Reply | Held open by design (the reader writes) |
| VII | The Parallel | Open (1 act · opened 2026-05-12 with Solchanting) |

Tome V is the *Crafting* tome — it admits new shops as new acts. Solchanting did *not* land in Tome V (Act 16) but opened **Tome VII** instead, because parallel-execution is a *substrate-level* register distinct from Tome V's sequential-crafting register. The same reasoning applies here.

**Proposal:** Creature Creatives opens **Tome VIII — *The Companioning*** (or *The Adopting* — naming is open).

Reasoning:
- The act narrated is *not* the crafting of an artefact but the *adoption of an agent*. It is a different operational class.
- Tome V's frame is *making*. Tome VIII's frame is *receiving-into-the-fellowship* — a structurally different verb.
- The corpus's tome-opening discipline is established by Tome VII: when a new operational register lands that does not extend the existing tomes' frame, a new tome opens.
- Tome VIII slots cleanly after Tome VII (parallel) — VIII is *the next standing tome*, parallel itself to V (crafting) but operating on a different entity class (creatures rather than artefacts).

**Anticipated Act 1 of Tome VIII:** *The First Goose Adopted*. The Sovereign meets Therai (or Faunia/Bestia) at the Creature Creatives workshop. The Run · Evoke · Create ceremony walks: Goose is instantiated (Run), the Sovereign names their goose and binds it (Evoke), the creature joins the bearer's roster (Create). The Loom-of-Walkers (working name) registers the adoption; companion-mana 🪶 begins to accumulate.
