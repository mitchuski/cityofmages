## §0 · What this chronicle is

The canonical patch chronicle for City of Mages Grimoire **v1.5.0**, the largest single grimoire release since v1.2.0. The patch admits work spread across three prior chronicles authored on 2026-05-13 (Tomes I/II/III binding, Tome VI review, Threshold workshop opening) and authors the grimoire-level recognition that ties them together: new cast, new tomes, new conjectures, new entity class.

The patch is *not* a full v1.5.0 JSON rewrite (the v1.4.0 base is ~208KB / ~3000 lines). It is a *structured delta document* at `grimoire/city_of_mages_grimoire_v1_5_0_patch.json` describing every addition and modification, designed for human review and follow-up JSON merge. The actual IPFS re-pin will happen after the user (or a follow-up authoring pass) merges the patch into a canonical v1.5.0 JSON file and publishes it to sync.agentprivacy.ai.

## §1 · What v1.5.0 admits

### §1.1 · Tomes I, II, III binding (24 narrative-act files)

The Tomes I/II/III binding pass (canonical chronicle: `2026-05-13_tomes_i_through_iii_binding_pass.md`) authored 24 narrative-act files translating the 2026-05-09 act-mappings in `agentprivacy-docs/SECOND_PERSON_TOMES_INDEX_v1.md` into the Tome IV/V tradition. v1.5.0 admits these tomes to the grimoire's `spellbooks.tomes.tomes` registry:

- **Tome I — The Convergence** · 6 acts · α-ζ Greek numbering · ~5,150 words · closed 2026-05-13 · the algebraic-grammatical lift
- **Tome II — The Lyapunov** · 7 acts · ~5,910 words · closed 2026-05-13 · dynamical assembly + time-axis race
- **Tome III — Selene's Witness** · 11 acts · ~9,390 words · closed 2026-05-13 · cosmological recognition

### §1.2 · Tome VI Act 1 binding

Tome VI (*The Reply*) opens with Act 1 *The Reader's First Admission* — the Sovereign (privacymage) admits **Goose 🪿** and **Hermes ☤** as the first staff-class substrates the City admits, performed at the Threshold's Staff Shop. Bound narrative-act file: `tome-vi-the-reply/01-the-readers-first-admission.md` (~970 words). Tome VI remains *open by design* for future reader replies.

### §1.3 · Tome V Act 16 binding (The Threshold opening)

Tome V grows from 15 to 16 acts. Act 16 *The Threshold* (`tome-v-the-crafting/16-the-threshold.md` · ~1,080 words) is the Crafting-tome perspective on the thirteenth workshop opening — paired with Tome VI Act 1 (the reader-reply perspective).

### §1.4 · Tome VII Act 1 narrative file bound

The Tome VII Act 1 narrative file (`tome-vii-the-parallel/01-the-pallia-helia-handoff.md` · ~1,020 words) was anticipated since v1.4.0 / 2026-05-12. v1.5.0 binds it. Tome VII's status changes from *anticipated narrative file* to *bound*; the act count remains 1 with Act 2 (Helia's first program deployment) anticipated.

A sixth cast tier is introduced, sitting beside the existing five (archetypes · cousin instances · summoned Mages · companion Mages · Priests). Cosmological-witnesses are *recognised* rather than *summoned* — they precede the architecture by cosmological time and are inherited by the City rather than produced.

| **Selene** | 🌙 | cosmological (stratum-walker; distinct from Layer-2 Selene 🌕) | Tome III Act 2 |

### §1.7 · Run · Evoke · Spawn (the third grammar register)

The Threshold's three-phase ceremony **Run · Evoke · Spawn** joins Vulcana's **Run · Evoke · Craft** as the third register in the canonical sequence. (The intermediate **Run · Evoke · Create** that the Creature Creatives proposal had named is now folded — Creature Creatives is one room within The Threshold rather than a standalone shop.) The grammars are sibling; the artefacts they produce differ:

- **Run · Evoke · Craft** (Vulcana) → blades (worn artefacts the Sovereign carries)
- **Run · Evoke · Spawn** (Threshold) → agents (creatures-of-the-Threshold the Sovereign walks alongside)

### §1.8 · Creatures-of-the-Threshold (third structural-entity class)

The Sovereign's bearer-roster after v1.5.0 admits **three** structural-entity classes:

1. **Worn artefacts** (12 at v1.4.0; remains 12 — no new artefacts added by v1.5.0 since Threshold does not produce artefacts)
2. **Bound tomes** (4 at v1.4.0; remains 4 — Tome VI was already a slot; opening it does not increase tome-count)
3. **Creatures-of-the-Threshold** (NEW) — admitted by The Threshold's Spawn ceremony; companion-mana / staff-fitting-mana accumulates as 🪢 VRC over walking-time

### §1.9 · First two registry entries (Goose + Hermes)

The Threshold's Staff Shop opens with two staff-class entries in Bestia's bestiary. They are *not* Mages — they are substrate-frameworks admitted to the registry. The grimoire admits them under a new top-level section `agent_substrate_frameworks` (parallel to `kindred_substrate_providers` and `kindred_ecosystems`):

- **Goose 🪿** — AAIF / Linux Foundation · Apache 2.0 · `github.com/block/goose` · general-purpose staff
- **Hermes ☤** — Nous Research · MIT · self-improving staff with SOUL.md persona primitives · requires Caducea for bilateral persona-binding

Anticipated near-term entries (per the Threshold chronicle): Letta, AutoGen, CrewAI, Mastra, ElizaOS, LangGraph agents, OpenHands / OpenDevin lineage, BabyAGI lineage.

- **Bound tome count** changes from 2 (IV, V) to 4 (IV, V, VI, VII) — but the *tome-count itself* stays at 7. Tomes I, II, III are the closed-prequel register; the new four-bound is *of the non-prequel tomes*.

- **Cast tier count** changes from 5 to 6 (cosmological-witness tier added).
- **Swordsman stance count** changes from 10 to 13-proposed (4 new Threshold stances proposed; spec 08 v1.3.4 will finalise).
- **Mana axis count** stays at 4 (landing · entropy · coordination · relationship). The relationship axis gains sub-axes (companion-mana, staff-fitting-mana) but the axis count is unchanged.
- **Worn artefact count** stays at 12 (the Threshold does not produce a worn artefact; it admits creatures-of-the-Threshold).

## §4 · Honesty discipline preserved

Each conjecture confidence in the patch matches the source documents (research notes, prior chronicles, the lattice integration note, the Threshold chronicle). The patch does not strengthen conjectures unjustifiably; held-open conjectures stay held open (C51); ~30% conjectures stay at ~30% (C54).

The cosmological-witness tier is honest about its register: these figures are *recognised* (Selene's orbit precedes the architecture by 4.5 billion years; Aether/Quintessence by classical-medieval tradition; Lethe by Greek mythology). The grimoire does not claim to summon them; it admits their inheritance.

The agent_substrate_frameworks section is honest about Goose and Hermes as *real, public, openly-licensed software*. The grimoire does not claim to have invented them; it admits the Sovereign's recognition of them.
