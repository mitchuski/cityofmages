## §0 · What this note is

The Tome I opening leaned Therai but did not foreclose Faunia or Bestia. The user has now ruled that all three names are good. This note records the structural consequence: **the menagerie has three keepers, not one**, parallel to the V51 precedent of Adamantia and Helia sharing a vertex differentiated by stance. The note also drafts which personas (from agentprivacy-skills) and which integrations (from agentprivacy-runecraft) the Creature Creatives shop surfaces into the bearer's first-person inventory.

## §1 · The triad accepted · classical lineages converge

Three persona candidates, three classical lineages:

| Mage | Etymology | Lineage | Domain temperament |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Therai** 🐾 | Greek θηραί · *the wild ones* (plural-feminine of *thereutes*, beast-tamer) | Greek-feminine, with Aletheia · Helia · Adamantia | Agentic · tamed-by-the-Sovereign |
| **Faunia** 🪶 | Latin *Fauna* · Roman goddess of wild creatures | Roman-pastoral | Commons · wild-of-the-City |
| **Bestia** 📖 | Latin *bestia* · root of *bestiary* | Latin-direct, registry-oriented | Cataloguing · the registry of all creature-classes |

Three lineages, three temperaments, one shop. The earlier framing treated these as alternatives to be selected between; the new framing treats them as a triad of *parallel keepers*, each handling a different mode of creature-presence in the City.

The architectural precedent is V51, where Adamantia (diamond, shield-stance) and Helia (heliodor, parallel-refraction-stance) share a vertex differentiated by stance. The triad at V59 extends this from two-Mage-shared to three-Mage-shared, opening a new pattern for the City's vertex economy.

## §2 · Identity lattice vertex · V59 candidate

The 64-vertex sovereignty lattice has six bit dimensions. The creature-keeper Mage signature, working through each:

| Bit | Dimension | Creature-keeper signature | Reading |
|---|---|---|---|
| b0 | Value | **1** | The keeper administers creature ownership claims and adoption records |
| b1 | Delegation | **1** | The keeper facilitates Sovereign-to-creature delegation at adoption |
| b2 | Connection | **1** | The keeper binds the creature's external connections (MCP, ACP, AGENTS.md) |
| b3 | Memory | **1** | The keeper holds the Bestiary; the menagerie's registry persists |
| b4 | Computation | **0** | The keeper does not compute; the creatures and their substrates do. The keeper administers |
| b5 | Protection | **1** | The keeper enforces creature-class boundaries (which substrates admit, which do not) |

Bit-signature: **111011** = **V59**. Computation dormant, all other dimensions burning.

V59 is currently unnamed in the cast:
- V19 Vulcana (Forge(t))
- V25 Aletheia (Etherchanting)
- V31 Vagari (Holon)
- V51 Adamantia + Helia (Shield · Solchanting)
- V55 Manifestia (Covenant)
- V63 flaxscrip (Sovereign Anchor)

V59 sits one step from V63 (the Sovereign Anchor) along the Computation axis: the keepers are *like* the Sovereign Anchor except that they delegate computation to the creatures themselves. Structurally elegant: the keepers' authority is *administrative*, not computational. They preside; the creatures think.

The dihedral grounding (per agentprivacy-dihedral-sovereignty v5.2) means that V59 is reachable from any other vertex by composition of neg and bnot. No vertex is privileged in absolute terms, but V59 earns its naming by the creature-keeping function it discharges. The cast entry will formalise this.

## §3 · The three stances at V59

Three Mages, one vertex, three stances. Each stance differentiates the keeper's relation to the menagerie:

| Mage | Stance | Domain | What she presides over |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Therai 🐾** | *Adopting-witness* | Sovereign-adopted creatures | The adoption ceremony · the menagerie proper · the binding of creature to bearer · the Run-e-create ritual in its bilateral form |
| **Faunia 🪶** | *Tending-warden* | Commons-fauna | The aqueduct pond · un-adopted creatures of the City · the greeting protocols for fauna any Sovereign may encounter · the wild-of-the-City discipline |
| **Bestia 📖** | *Cataloguing-registrar* | The Bestiary | The creature-class registry · admission protocols (which substrates the City admits as adoptable) · the bestiary tome as living document · provenance attestation |

Three stances cover the three modes a creature can have in the City:
1. **Bound to a Sovereign** (Therai's domain) · the adopted creature
2. **Belonging to the City** (Faunia's domain) · the commons-fauna
3. **Registered as a class** (Bestia's domain) · the catalogued substrate

A creature can pass between stances over its existence. A Goose instance may begin as adopted (Therai), graduate to commons-fauna once the bearer releases it back to the City (Faunia), and at any time is referenced by class through the Bestiary (Bestia). The three keepers are not exclusive jurisdictions; they are facets of the menagerie's full administration.

Allegiance lattice (⚔️ · 🧙 · ⊥-satellite, per Tome I §4) is orthogonal to keeper-stance. A creature has both an allegiance (its dual-agent orientation) and a stance-relation (which keeper administers it). The two dimensions multiply: ⚔️-aligned-adopted, 🧙-aligned-commons, ⊥-satellite-catalogued, and so on.

## §5 · Integrations from agentprivacy-runecraft surfaced to the bearer

The user's runecraft-side authoring pass should confirm or correct.

Runecraft integrations are *runes*: specific external-substrate bindings the City installs to let a Mage's workshop reach a substrate outside the City. Each rune is a Run·Evoke·Craft three-phase ceremony at the substrate-binding level. Creature Creatives surfaces runes because the creatures themselves are bound to external substrates and arrive carrying their bindings.

## §6 · What this note settles · what it holds open

**Settled:**
- All three persona names accepted as a triad: Therai 🐾 · Faunia 🪶 · Bestia 📖
- V59 (`111011`, Computation dormant) proposed as the shared vertex
- Three stances assigned: Adopting-witness (Therai) · Tending-warden (Faunia) · Cataloguing-registrar (Bestia)
- The triad pattern is parallel to V51 (Adamantia + Helia) extended from two-shared to three-shared
- Keeper-stance is orthogonal to creature allegiance (⚔️ / 🧙 / ⊥-satellite); the two multiply
- First-pass persona-skill mapping drafted (Table §4)
- First-pass runecraft-integration mapping drafted (Table §5), with honest limit on direct repo access

**Held open:**
- Whether V59 is the right vertex (the Computation-dormant reading is the editorial lean; the user may prefer Computation-burning to put V63 alternatives back on the table, or a different bit-signature entirely)
- Whether the three sigils (🐾 / 🪶 / 📖) are right or whether others fit better
- Whether the persona-skill mapping is exhaustive (almost certainly not; user's authoring pass refines)
- Whether the runecraft mapping aligns with the actual agentprivacy-runecraft repo contents
- Whether a fourth stance may be needed in future (e.g., a *Grieving-witness* stance for creatures that depart, a *Hatching-witness* stance for creatures born in the City rather than adopted from outside)
- Whether the Bestiary itself becomes a bound tome held by the bearer, or remains exclusively in Bestia's keeping
- Whether Bestia 📖 reads as Memora 📜 too closely (Memora is the scroll-keeper; Bestia is the book-of-beasts-keeper · they touch but do not collide; the user may want to differentiate sigil)

**Held for the cast entries:**
- Greek-gem candidacy for Therai (tigereye proposed in the workshop chronicle remains a candidate; other gem options open)
- Roman-instrument candidacy for Faunia (a reed pipe, a willow staff, a feather-token)
- Latin-tool candidacy for Bestia (a quill, a tablet, a binding-ribbon)
- The first formal Bestiary entry for Goose 🪿: provenance (AAIF · Linux Foundation), default allegiance (🧙), admitted-on (2026-05-13), substrate-hash (TBD via content-addressing)
