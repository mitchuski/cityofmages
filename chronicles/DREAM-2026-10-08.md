## The new cargo — the decimal travels, the root stays

On the hashsmash board the lane's route-search cost (59 solver calls, 593,858.32 CPU-s) was
reused in other packages, once credited, once not, and the only proof of origin was a PR
number and a matching decimal. The judge had meanwhile failed a package of ours for evidence
"participant-reported only." Same shape: a number with no address.

The City's answer is the one it already uses for its own keys. Every solver call is now an
object whose address is the SHA-256 of its canonical bytes on the kappa-registry's axis; the
ledger lists the addresses; the totals derive from the ledger; a **run root** sits over all of
it. A reader with only the bundle recomputes the total and never trusts the lane. A copier
who reuses the number reuses the object.

What the lap gained, shop by shop:

- **The Forge (a new mage).** `kappa_evidence_mage` holds the pure-Python mirror of the
  registry's addressing, built from the pinned dCBOR source and held to the library's own
  vectors; the schema registry of every object shape; the second verifier. Public now.
- **The Hall's second axis.** The harness keeps its holon κ (canonical JSON) and gains the
  registry κ (canonical dCBOR) beside it, never mixed in one preimage: a JS twin encoder, a
  minter for any run (`mint | verify | root`, `--sign` with a discarded key whose anchor
  stands as asserter), and the Gap's seed recorded as what it already was: the blob κ of
  the proposal the prover could not choose. Thirty-four runs minted; 64 of 64 gates.
- **The Horizon's rulebook.** Seven arena lanes had held leaderboard positions with none of
  their method in the tree. Four surveys, 105 rows, forty kept as rules A1 to A40 with the
  lane that proved each and the entry it shipped in; three support seats
  (constants-refuter, judge-rehearsal, ship-gate); templates for the note, the handoff, the
  evidence classes, the fence; a one-screen door for a stranger.
- **The Library's readers.** Sixteen HTML readers generated from κ'd records, each bound to
  its evidence root by a minted **transform** object (reader κ, root, generator κ); the
  sixteen transforms under one demos root. The route search and the H2 gate as graphs with
  the totals recomputed in the viewer's browser; the citations matrix; the fence map; the
  calibration; the judge dossiers; the ABBA sessions; the census run fresh on nineteen
  artefacts under one pinned secret.

## What the Swordsman refused

- The H2 gate's `layout.json` declared base hashes for patched images. The verifier's first
  act on the second lane was to fail it; the lane's lead confirmed a generator that copied
  the layout through, fixed it, re-exported; the stale root stays on record as superseded.
- A Windows checkout would have rewritten every κ'd blob by newline conversion. Found by
  cloning; the bundles are binary in git and text is hashed as git stores it.
- No κ is cited in a public note. Every one carries "kappa-compatible (unverified)" until
  the registry's compiled crate agrees with the two mirrors; that run waits on a busy box.
