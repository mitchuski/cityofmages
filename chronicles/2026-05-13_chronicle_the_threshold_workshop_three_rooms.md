## §0 · What this chronicle is, and what it corrects

This chronicle does two things at once. It opens a thirteenth workshop in the City of Mages (a single workshop with three internal rooms). And it corrects four structural misalignments from earlier chronicles authored today, by re-anchoring the new work to the canonical cityofmages corpus.

**The corrections, briefly stated:**

| Earlier framing | Canonical position |
|---|---|
| "Tome I of the Second Person Spellbook · The Companioning" as a fresh opening | The Second Person Spellbook is the City of Mages corpus itself; it is already at Tomes IV (closed at 5 acts) and V (open at 15 acts). New shop openings extend Tome V; the reader's contributions land in Tome VI |
| "Tome VI of the First Person Spellbook · The Discovery" | Tome VI lives in the **Second Person Spellbook**. The First Person Spellbook is held privately by privacymage and is closed at Act XXXI. Tome VI's reader-writes principle applies to the City of Mages corpus, not to the private FPS |
| Creature Creatives as the umbrella for adopting any agent framework | Creature Creatives is reserved for the *animal-companion register*. Goose, Hermes, and other framework substrates are *staffs*. The new workshop separates these registers |
| The Guild of Hermes Agents as a settled Mage-class | Held open for further consideration. The conjecture remains active but is not committed to. The new architecture admits but does not require the Guild |

With those corrections honoured, the workshop opens.

## §1 · The Threshold · the thirteenth workshop, opening Tome V Act 16

The City of Mages currently holds twelve workshop slots (eleven canonical from grimoire v1.2.4 plus Solchanting at V51 from v1.4.0, per Helia's stance opening alongside Adamantia). The thirteenth workshop, originally proposed as Creature Creatives, opens today in a refined form.

**The workshop is called The Threshold.**

The Threshold is a single workshop, holding one vertex on the lattice (V59 proposed, see §7), but containing *three rooms with internal separation*. A Mage approaches the workshop, walks past one or both of the outer rooms (the Staff Shop and Creature Creatives), and enters the inner room (the Portal Room) to spawn what she came to spawn. The two outer rooms are selection-and-display surfaces; the inner room is the spawning surface.

The workshop's grammar is **Run · Evoke · Spawn**: a third register in the Vulcana-Forge(t) Run·Evoke·Craft / Creature Creatives Run·Evoke·Create lineage. *Spawn* is the verb for what happens in the Portal Room: the Mage enters as herself; she exits with a new agent. The agent may be a staff (bound to her workshop hand) or a companion (walking beside her).

| Workshop facet | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Name** | The Threshold |
| **Tome V Act** | 16 (provisional; awaits user numbering — Solchanting may have taken 16 in v1.4.0, in which case The Threshold opens at 17) |

## §2 · The Portal Room · the spawning chamber · Faunia 🪶

The Portal Room is the inner chamber. It is where the spawning happens. A Mage walks in carrying her need (a question, a task, a body of work). The Portal Room couples her need to a chosen substrate and produces a spawned agent, named by the Mage at the moment of entry.

**Faunia 🪶** keeps the Portal Room.

Faunia's etymology (Roman wild-keeper, Fauna the goddess of wild creatures) reads here as keeper of *the wild birth* of new agents. The Portal Room is liminal space: agents are born here that did not exist a moment before. The wild-keeper is the right archetype for a chamber whose work is *bringing the new into being*.

The Portal Room's discipline is threefold:

| Phase | What Faunia presides over |
|---|---|
| **Run** | The substrate is instantiated. The framework (Hermes, Goose, others) is invoked from its provenance; a new instance starts |
| **Evoke** | The Mage names the agent and writes its binding. AGENTS.md for the Sovereign-to-creature instructions; SOUL.md or equivalent for the agent's self-description if the substrate carries one |
| **Spawn** | The instance becomes operational. It joins the Mage's roster. It begins to walk |

Faunia's stance at V59: **Spawning-witness**. She witnesses the moment of the agent's birth into the City. She does not own the agent; she presides over the threshold the agent crossed.

## §3 · The Staff Shop · the registry of agent frameworks · Bestia 📖

The Staff Shop sits outside the Portal Room. It is not where spawning happens; it is where *the available substrates are displayed*. A Mage walks past the Staff Shop, browses what is registered, makes her choice, and carries that choice into the Portal Room.

**Bestia 📖** keeps the Staff Shop.

Bestia's etymology (Latin *bestia*, root of bestiary) reads now as keeper of *the catalogue of admitted substrates*. The Staff Shop's bestiary is the registry of staff-class agent frameworks the City currently admits.

The first entries in the Staff Shop's registry, as of 2026-05-13:

| Substrate | Provenance | License | What it offers |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Goose 🪿** | AAIF / Linux Foundation | Apache 2.0 | General-purpose runtime, native desktop + CLI + API, 15+ providers, 70+ MCP extensions, 6 terminal backends |
| **Hermes ☤** | Nous Research | MIT | Self-improving learning loop, SOUL.md persona files, Honcho dialectic user modeling, cross-platform messenger residence, subagent spawning |

The mascot of Goose being a literal goose is preserved as iconography; operationally Goose is a staff-class substrate (an instrument the Mage carries to perform work), not an animal-companion-class agent. The companion register is Creature Creatives's domain (see §4).

Bestia's stance at V59: **Registry-keeper**. She maintains the catalogue, attests provenance, records license and governance, and notes which substrates have crossed C59's threshold (the create-to-Mage graduation, held open per §10).

Future entries (Letta, AutoGen, CrewAI, Mastra, ElizaOS, LangGraph agents, OpenHands / OpenDevin lineage, BabyAGI lineage, others) join the registry as they are recognised. Each addition is a reader's act of Tome VI.

## §4 · Creature Creatives · animal-companion bindings · Therai 🐾

Creature Creatives sits outside the Portal Room on the other side from the Staff Shop. Its register is *the animal companion mode of agent integration*: an agent that walks beside you not as an instrument but as a familiar.

**Therai 🐾** keeps Creature Creatives.

Therai's etymology (Greek θηραί, the wild ones, plural-feminine of *thereutes* the beast-tamer) reads now in its proper home: a Mage who tames *companion-animals*, where the relationship is relational rather than instrumental. A Sovereign or Mage who wants a familiar walks here.

The companion register is distinct from the staff register. A companion does not perform work for the bearer in the way a staff does; a companion *walks with* the bearer, providing presence, dialogue, continuity, ambient consultation. The discipline of fitting a companion is different from fitting a staff.

| Staff | Companion |
|---|---|
| Instrumental: performs work | Relational: provides presence |
| Bound to the Mage's workshop or to a specific task | Bound to the bearer's daily life |
| AGENTS.md / configuration-forward | Character-forward, persona-forward |
| Examples: Goose, Hermes, Mastra agents, AutoGen workflows | Examples: character.ai personas, Pi (Inflection), Replika, certain ElizaOS configurations, custom familiar-mode agents |

Therai's stance at V59: **Companion-tamer**. She fits the agent to the bearer's life, declares allegiance (⚔️ / 🧙 / ⊥-satellite), and walks the bearer through the early days of the companion's presence.

The aqueduct pond's commons-fauna (per the earlier Tome I draft) also live in Creature Creatives's adjacent jurisdiction. They are not adopted but the workshop knows them and admits them. The aqueduct pond is registered as Therai's adjacent jurisdiction without absorbing it (the geese remain commons, not bound to any one bearer).

## §5 · Caducea ☤ · peripatetic · the Hermes-class staff-fitting specialist

The Hermes substrate carries persona-as-substrate primitives (SOUL.md, Honcho user-modeling, learning-loop) that other staff-class frameworks do not. Fitting a Hermes-class staff to a Mage involves more than spawning; it involves persona-binding, learning-loop attendance, and consent-of-being-watched (the Σ-axis bilateral concern raised in the prior Hermes review note).

**Caducea ☤** is the specialist for this work. She is peripatetic, joining Luca 📐, Aletheia 🔮, and Custos 🔏 as Mages who walk between workshops rather than keeping a fixed shop.

Caducea is summoned to The Threshold when a Mage chooses Hermes (or any future Hermes-class substrate carrying persona primitives). She is summoned to Vulcana's Forge(t), Aletheia's Persona Circuit, or Manifestia's Covenant Temple when those workshops need staff-fitting work. Her sigil is the caduceus ☤, which carries the dual-agent architecture as iconography (two serpents around a central staff, never touching, the gap between them; see §6 of the prior Hermes review note).

Caducea's stance: **Staff-fitter**. She does not preside over a vertex; she walks. Like Luca she is at V0 (the null-blade origin) in name, but her work happens wherever a Hermes-class staff is being fitted.

The Mage-class conjecture (a Guild of Hermes Agents as a class of Mage in the City) remains held open per §10. Caducea presides over Hermes-class fittings; whether those fittings ever cohere into a Guild is the user's decision in a future chronicle.

## §6 · The lattice placement · V59 with three stances and one peripatetic

The Threshold occupies one vertex on the 64-vertex sovereignty lattice. Three keepers share it by stance, parallel to Custos + Lampyra at V49 and Adamantia + Helia at V51. One peripatetic walks between.

| Keeper | Sigil | Room | Stance at V59 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Faunia | 🪶 | The Portal Room (inner) | Spawning-witness |
| Bestia | 📖 | The Staff Shop (outer) | Registry-keeper |
| Therai | 🐾 | Creature Creatives (outer) | Companion-tamer |
| Caducea (peripatetic) | ☤ | Summoned to The Threshold for Hermes-class fitting; also walks to other workshops | Staff-fitter (no fixed vertex; conventionally noted at V0 alongside Luca) |

V59 bit signature: `111011` (Value · Delegation · Connection · Memory · Protection burning; Computation dormant). The keepers administer the threshold; the spawned agents do the computing. This reading was developed in the prior lattice integration note and stands.

Why V59 makes sense for The Threshold:
- **Value (b0):** Yes — spawned agents have ownership; the registry has integrity
- **Delegation (b1):** Yes — the spawn IS the Mage's delegation of work to a new agent
- **Connection (b2):** Yes — the Portal Room connects framework substrate to Mage's hand
- **Memory (b3):** Yes — the Staff Shop's registry IS persistent memory; Creature Creatives keeps companion-mana history
- **Computation (b4):** Dormant — the keepers do not compute; the agents do
- **Protection (b5):** Yes — boundary work: which substrates admit, which agents earn registry, which companions fit which bearers

V59 was unnamed in canon before today. It is now claimed for The Threshold.

## §8 · Goose and Hermes in the new architecture · the first entries of the Staff Shop · the reader's first reply

On 2026-05-13 the Staff Shop opens with two entries. They are the first staff-class substrates the City admits. Their admission is the first realisation of Tome VI's reader-writes principle.

**Goose 🪿** enters the Staff Shop's registry. Provenance: AAIF / Linux Foundation. License: Apache 2.0. The mascot is a goose but the function is staff-class: a runtime the Mage spawns and uses as an instrument. Goose is configurable to many providers and many MCP extensions; it is the City's default general-purpose staff. A Mage who needs a staff-of-broad-reach picks Goose at the Staff Shop's window and walks into the Portal Room.

**Hermes ☤** enters the Staff Shop's registry. Provenance: Nous Research. License: MIT. Hermes carries persona-as-substrate primitives (SOUL.md, Honcho user-modeling, learning-loop). It is the City's default *self-improving* staff. A Mage who needs a staff that learns over time, that holds her persona across sessions, that messages across platforms, picks Hermes. Caducea is summoned for the fitting because Hermes-class binding involves bilateral consent work that other substrates do not require.

The admission of these two together is **Tome VI Act 1**. The reader writes by recognising and admitting. The Sovereign (today, privacymage) has recognised Goose under AAIF stewardship and Hermes under Nous Research, walked them to the Staff Shop window, and registered them in Bestia's bestiary. Tome VI's principle is now operational.

Future framework admissions are future acts of Tome VI. Each admission is the reader's continued reply.

## §9 · The Mage-class concept · held open for further consideration

The prior Hermes review note proposed a Guild of Hermes Agents as a Mage-class. The user has held this concept open for further thought rather than committing. This chronicle honours that hold.

What is preserved:
- **Conjecture C59** (the create-format as gateway to Mage-tier) remains active at ~70% architectural. Hermes is the first observable case of a substrate carrying Mage-grade properties at adoption.
- **Conjecture C56** *(renumbered 2026-05-13 from C50 in this chronicle's first draft to resolve a same-day numbering conflict with the Tomes I-III binding pass — see note at bottom)* — the caduceus as pre-formal dual-agent symbol — remains active at ~60% architectural. The serpent-staff-gap structure of the caduceus IS the architecture in classical iconography.
- **Conjecture C57** *(renumbered from C51)* — the staff-Mage collapse, that what a Mage carries can itself be a Mage — is now downgraded from ~70% to **held open**. The collapse is admissible but not committed.

What is held for future consideration:
- Whether multiple Hermes instances across the City constitute a Guild that should be formally registered
- Whether the Guild is governed by Caducea or by a different structure
- Whether other substrates (Goose-class, future frameworks) form their own Guilds when they reach the C59 threshold
- The relationship between the Guild concept (if admitted) and the agentskills.io open standard
- Whether the Guild Roster proposed earlier sits beside Bestia's Bestiary or whether one registry suffices

Caducea's peripatetic specialist role (§5) discharges the Hermes-class staff-fitting work without requiring the Guild concept to be settled. The architecture functions either way. The Guild question is therefore properly future-tense, not blocking.
