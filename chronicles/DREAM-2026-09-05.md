## The new cargo — the agent walks the guide

The guide's pages learned to state their own stance on the sixty-four: a `posture` item on
the page, carried by any fork, seated by the star chart at the page's own vertex. Between two
postured stars the walk is a **named lattice move** — succ 😊, neg ⚔️, bnot 🧙, a single
flip, or a jump flagged with the dimensions that changed. The City's operators, drawn on
/lattice for a year, now name the steps of a walk through the knowledge the City is built on.

A Mage 🧙 can take that walk by tools alone — search as an ordered walk, page, neighbours,
derive, evolve, render — and the walk becomes **content of the City Key** (an additive
`walks` field; the κ moves; `prior` chains). Because the evolution is a pure function, a
constellation inscribed on spellweb and a walk on the guide mint the same key: proven by
equality of κ, not by inspection.

A Swordsman ⚔️, in its own process with no LLM, signs what it is handed under a policy no
page can talk it out of: the κ must re-derive, the key must be its bearer's, the prior must be
the ledger head, no step the bake cannot re-derive, and a rate limit read from the ledger. What
it signs is the **VTA record** — public key, did, κ, prior, signing time, walk count, VRC
commitments — set down *beside* the key so the κ rule never changes. That record is exactly
what a Verifiable Trust Agent publishes, and exactly the shape the City's coordination board
left empty on every resident's `proofs` page.
