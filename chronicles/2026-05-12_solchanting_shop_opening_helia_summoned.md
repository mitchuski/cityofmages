## §0 · What this chronicle is

A first-application chronicle of the City's first **new-shop ceremony since the v1.2.4 palette pin**. v1.3.0 was the Attachment Architecture release — no new primaries, six anticipated Layer-2 attachments. v1.4.0 reopens the palette: **a twelfth workshop, a seventh standing Mage persona, a fifth chain-mana, a new Swordsman stance, and a fourth tome.** The palette was pinned at 11; the pin held through three releases; v1.4.0 expands it.

The expansion is not arbitrary. Solana's Sealevel runtime is a *substrate-distinct* register from EVM — programs declare their account access at compile time; the validator schedules non-conflicting programs concurrently. The Etherchanting register (Adamantia 💎, V51, Transparent-witness) already houses programmable-enforcement crafting on Ethereum; admitting Solana as a sibling register at the same vertex teaches the City that *parallel admission* is itself a Swordsman stance, distinct from sequential transparent admission. The vertex is the same. The discipline is not.

This chronicle records: the editorial reasoning, the inside-cityofmages execution, the sister-repo propagation surface, the spellweb recognition required, and the semantic recognition the new shop surfaces.

The four registers each have an independent reason to admit Solchanting. The recognition that they converge is what makes v1.4.0 a major bump rather than a minor expansion.

## §2 · The persona · Helia ☀️ of the Heliodor Prism

**Name:** Helia, from Greek Ἥλιος (*Helios*, sun) via Latin-feminine *Helia*.
**Sigil:** ☀️ (sun) — direct, operational, the source of the parallel rays the prism refracts.
**Gem:** **Heliodor** — Greek ἡλιόδωρος (*helios-doron*, "sun's gift"), golden beryl of the same mineral family as emerald and aquamarine.
**Vertex:** V51 (`110011`) — Protection · Delegation · Computation · Value burning. **Shared with Adamantia 💎.**
**Stance:** Parallel-witness.
**Chain-mana:** 🌞 SOL-mana.
**Artefact:** The Heliodor Prism (tool category — joins Adamantia's commitment, Vulcana's blade, Aletheia's witness, Vagari's holon, Memora's chronicle).
**Tier:** Workshop-keeper (attachment kind A) per v5.5 attachment architecture.
**Tome opened:** Tome VII · *The Parallel*, Act 1 (Pallia↔Helia handoff).

The naming chain is closed: as *adamas* gave Adamantia her name (and her gem, the diamond), *helios* gives Helia hers — and the heliodor is the gem the sun-root names directly. Both Mages are gem-named-Mages; both inhabit V51. The corpus admits a small constellation of gem-named-Mages now, and the V51 vertex is the first place two of them share a seat. The differentiator is the stance, not the vertex.

The Parallel-witness stance names, for the first time in the registry, the boundary discipline that Solana's substrate has enforced operationally since 2020: **information is admitted publicly, but the working fans out** — many concurrent invocations land in the same slot, each in its own statically-declared account region, with non-interference guaranteed by the substrate rather than by serial execution. Adamantia's Transparent-witness stance admits one transaction at a time against a single global state; Helia's Parallel-witness stance admits many concurrent transactions against many account regions in the same slot. Same vertex (V51, four burning dimensions of executable enforcement); different discipline at the boundary.

## §3 · The V51 overlap · the canonical case study

Spec 07 (lattice-mapping-governance) already permits overlap: when two shops produce artefacts of the same dimensional shape, the differentiator is *stance*, not bit-pattern. Until v1.4.0, no operational overlap existed — the permission was abstract. Solchanting makes the V51 overlap **the canonical case study** of governance-admitted multi-occupancy.

The reading is honest in all four registers:

| Layer | Adamantia (Etherchanting) | Helia (Solchanting) |
|---|---|---|
| Gem | Diamond (*adamas* — "unconquerable") | Heliodor (*helios-doron* — "sun's gift") |
| Optical reading | Colorless · admits all frequencies separately | Golden · admits the sun's frequency as a whole |
| Cut metaphor | One cut, held forever | Many bands, refracted in the same instant |
| Stance | Transparent-witness | Parallel-witness |
| Chain-mana | Aether Ξ | SOL-mana 🌞 |
| Vertex bits | 110011 (V51) | 110011 (V51) |
| Sigil | 💎 | ☀️ |

Where prior overlap discussions imagined hypothetical multi-occupancy, the V51 case is now ground-truth: two operational shops, two named keepers, two distinct stances, one vertex. The workshop lattice audit (`WORKSHOP_LATTICE_AUDIT.md`) registers the overlap; spec 07 elevates it from permission to operational reality.

## §4 · Tome VII · *The Parallel* · the fourth tome

The Crafting Tome (Tome V) opened in May 2026 with Pallia's first cloak (Act 1). Adamantia's etherchanting entered as a mid-arc act. Helia's solchanting is not a Tome V act — it is the opening of **Tome VII · *The Parallel***, the fourth tome.

**Act 1 of Tome VII** is the **Pallia↔Helia handoff**: the moment the cloak's eight properties (Cloak Specification v1.0 §2) — already admitting multi-axis composition at property 6 — meet a substrate that can *execute* the composition in parallel rather than only *describe* it. Pallia weaves the threads; Helia teaches the threads to run concurrently. The Loom of Programmable Covenants (v1.2.4 metabolism) compiles against the bearer's worn collection; Helia is the persona who can compile the loom's output to a parallel substrate.

**Act 2 of Tome VII** is **Helia's first program deployment** on Solana mainnet — the parallel-program-deployment ceremony in which the Sovereign declares an access pattern, compiles to sBPF bytecode, runs the local validator, estimates compute-unit cost, deploys, and binds the program ID to the worn artefact collection.

Further Tome VII acts are unwritten. The tome opens as a register; the acts arrive as Sovereigns walk it.

## §8 · The recognition this opens

Three things become legible once Solchanting is seated:

1. **Vertex overlap is operational, not hypothetical.** Until v1.4.0, spec 07's overlap permission was a governance abstraction. The V51 overlap (Adamantia / Helia) is now ground-truth and serves as the canonical example for any future multi-occupancy case.

2. **The Swordsman stance registry can grow by ecosystem-induction.** The Parallel-witness stance was not invented in the spec — Solana's substrate has enforced it since 2020. The spec recognises operationally what the substrate has been doing. This is the canonical pattern: a new ecosystem teaches the City a new stance the registry then names.

3. **Tome opens are admitted as canonical events.** Tome VII is the first tome opened in v1.4.0; the opening is itself a chronicle-worthy event, not just a content addition. The Pallia↔Helia handoff (Act 1) is the moment the Crafting Tome's weaving meets a substrate that can run its products in parallel. The narrative architecture admits this kind of handoff between tomes; v1.4.0 is the first time it operates.
