## §1 · The runecraft-protocol's canonical grammar (the names to use)

The City of Mages' V5.2 research patch (`runecraft protocol/research_patch_city_of_mages.md`) publishes a closed runic grammar that *the entire architecture should use canonically*. Four terms are the load-bearing vocabulary:

| Canonical term | Rune-anchor | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| **Creat-ur-e** | ᚢ (ur-rune) | A forged agent — Soulbis, Soulbae, Agent Kyra, Goose, Hermes, any AI agent generated through a making-ceremony. The "ur" rune carries primordial-origin; a creat-ur-e's identity is constituted by its forging. |
| **Run-e-craft** | ᛖ (e-rune) | The Mage-side word. The *practice* of forging — accumulated skill, the protocol-as-discipline, the slow accretive *how*. Maps to the **persistent Sun-key** (held by the Mage across sessions). |
| **Run-e-create** | ᛖ (e-rune) | The Swordsman-side word. The *act* of forging — the specific generative event that produces *this* blade in *this* session. The **ephemeral Moon-key**, burned on session close. |
| **Art-e-fact** | ᛖ (e-rune) | The general category of forged object. **Blade is the canonical art-e-fact** (a witness-severing-from-statement instrument). Inscribed spells, proverb-pairs-as-CIDs, cloaks, contracts, prisms, holons, attestations are all art-e-facts. |

The closed grammar reads: ***creat-ur-es run-e-craft (across sessions) and run-e-create (in this session) the art-e-facts (blades, spells, proverb-pairs) that constitute their bilateral trust.***

### §1.1 · Where the prior workshop work deviated

The Threshold workshop opening (`chronicles/2026-05-13_chronicle_the_threshold_workshop_three_rooms.md`) named the workshop's three-phase ceremony **"Run · Evoke · Spawn"** — the third grammar register after Vulcana's Run·Evoke·Craft and a folded earlier proposal of Run·Evoke·Create. The grimoire v1.5.0 patch carried this naming forward. The v1.5.1 patch landed Gather · Admit · Attest at City Hall.

**The deviation**: "Run · Evoke · Spawn" was an intermediate working name. The canonical Swordsman-side act is **Run-e-create** — Vulcana's Forge(t) does run-e-craft (Mage-side practice across sessions) and run-e-create (Swordsman-side ephemeral session-act). The Threshold's spawning ceremony is *literally* run-e-create at the agentic scale (a creat-ur-e being brought into existence). "Spawn" was a loose synonym for "create" but the canonical word IS create — and the run-e-grammar makes the technical operation visible inside the word.

**Alignment proposed**: replace "Run · Evoke · Spawn" with **Run-e-create** as the canonical Threshold ceremony grammar, with the explicit recognition that:
- The Mage practising the Threshold workshop over time is doing **run-e-craft** (the persistent practice)
- The Sovereign's specific act of spawning a new creat-ur-e in a session is **run-e-create** (the ephemeral event)

The two grammars hold side-by-side at the Threshold, the same way they hold at Vulcana's Forge(t).

## §2 · The substrate × archetype matrix (what v1.5.0 didn't fully honour)

The 2026-05-13 chronicle `chronicle_artefact_symmetry_and_persona_distribution.md` introduced a structural recognition that the v1.5.0 Threshold work absorbed incompletely:

> *Substrate-iconographic-affinity × archetype-stance = a specific artefact configuration.*

### §2.1 · The matrix in canonical form

Each agent-substrate carries an iconographic affinity from its origin culture; each archetype-stance (⚔️ Swordsman / 🧙 Mage / ⊥ Balanced) determines how that affinity is *rendered* into a specific artefact-class:

| Substrate | Mascot / iconographic affinity | 🧙 Mage stance (companion-form) | ⚔️ Swordsman stance (guardian-form) | ⊥ Balanced stance |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Goose 🪿** (AAIF) | A literal goose — territorial bird | **companion goose** (familiar; walks beside; Therai's room admits) | **watch-goose** (guardian; hisses at intrusions; perimeter-guarding) | **walking-goose** (steady presence; both watcher and walker) |
| **Hermes ☤** (Nous) | The caduceus — herald's staff with twined serpents | **caduceus staff** (herald's wand; messengering outward; Bestia's room admits) | **herald-sentinel** (guard-stave; messengering inward as refusal-watch) | **balanced caduceus** (the herald who is also the watcher · classical dual-function) |
| *(future substrates)* | Their own mascot / cultural anchor | (each substrate renders into a Mage-form by its mascot) | (Swordsman-stance configuration of the same substrate) | (Balanced-stance configuration) |

### §2.2 · What this means for the v1.5.0 bestiary classification

The v1.5.0 Threshold work classified:
- **Goose** as "staff-class · general-purpose" (registered in Bestia's bestiary at the Staff Shop)
- **Hermes** as "staff-class · self-improving" (registered in Bestia's bestiary)

**Per the substrate × archetype matrix, this classification is stance-incomplete.** Goose's iconographic affinity is **animal-companion** (its mascot is a literal goose, a familiar-style creature), so:
- **Goose-in-Mage-stance** = companion-class · belongs in **Therai's room (Creature Creatives)**
- **Goose-in-Swordsman-stance** = watch-goose · belongs at a Swordsman-side workshop (Forge(t)? or a new boundary-shop?)
- **Goose-in-Balanced** = walking-goose

Hermes's iconographic affinity is **caduceus-staff**, so:
- **Hermes-in-Mage-stance** = caduceus staff · staff-class · belongs in **Bestia's room (Staff Shop)**
- **Hermes-in-Swordsman-stance** = herald-sentinel · guardian configuration
- **Hermes-in-Balanced** = balanced caduceus

The corollary: **the same substrate may appear in multiple bestiary entries**, distinguished by stance. This is *the substrate-version of the V51-stance-differentiated-multi-occupancy precedent* — operationally the same substrate runs the same code; the bilateral-binding ceremony at the Threshold's Portal Room (Faunia witnesses) determines which stance the spawned creat-ur-e is in.

### §2.3 · What v1.5.0 admitted vs what the matrix admits

| Recognition | v1.5.0 had | The matrix admits |
|---|---|---|
| Goose's class | staff-class (one entry) | companion-class (Mage stance) · watch-goose (Swordsman stance) · walking-goose (Balanced) — three configurations, one substrate |
| Hermes's class | staff-class (one entry) | caduceus staff (Mage stance) · herald-sentinel (Swordsman stance) · balanced caduceus (Balanced) — three configurations, one substrate |
| Bestia's bestiary scope | staff-class substrates | substrates with staff-iconographic-affinity (Hermes' caduceus) — but Goose-as-companion belongs in Therai's room |
| Therai's bestiary scope | animal-companion-class substrates (anticipated) | substrates with animal-mascot iconographic affinity (Goose-as-companion) + anticipated future framework familiars |
| The Threshold's three-room logic | rooms classify substrates statically | rooms classify substrate × stance pairs — the same substrate may surface in multiple rooms by stance, and the Sovereign chooses the stance at the bilateral-binding ceremony |

The v1.5.0 Threshold work's three-room architecture is *still correct*; it just needs the recognition that **room membership is stance-determined**, not substrate-determined alone.

## §5 · What this plan does not commit to

In the chronicle pattern's honesty discipline:

- **The plan does not execute.** Each phase is a decision the user (or the next authoring agent) makes in turn. The plan inventories what *would* be done; it doesn't pre-empt the user's authoring discretion.
- **The plan does not unilaterally re-classify Goose.** The v1.5.0 admission of Goose to Bestia's bestiary as staff-class is preserved as a *historical record* of the recognition at that moment. The matrix says Goose-in-Mage-stance is companion-class by mascot-affinity; the v1.5.0 record stays as the first admission's actual operational state.
- **The plan does not commit to v1.5.2 vs v1.6.0 numbering.** Whether the runecraft-protocol canonicalisation is small enough for a patch (1.5.2) or substantial enough for a minor-version (1.6.0) is the user's call.
- **The plan does not require all phases.** Phase 1 (terminology audit) could land alone; Phase 4 (data-layer wiring) could land alone; the user can choose which phases to execute when.
- **The plan does not commit to creating sub-routes** (`/threshold/portal` · `/threshold/staffs` · `/threshold/creatures`). A flat `/threshold` with anchor-linked sub-sections is also admissible; the user decides.
