## The new cargo, shop by shop

- **The Threshold, the pause runs again.** The First Person asked for October's results to be
  wiped, and the mechanism made them again in an afternoon. Two fresh gates on testnet, one for
  the demonstration rounds and one for the study, so the two kinds of round never share a day cap;
  then a third name, a registry of gates that keeps an entry only if the gate itself names the
  caller as its owner, and refused a stranger's claim live with the deposit returned. Sixty-five
  rounds over ten pairs of seats; no drain anywhere but at level zero of the ladder, where nothing
  but the policy and the proposal crossed. A result that can be regenerated on demand is a
  different kind of fact from one that must be kept.
- **The Weavers, a scroll for the pause.** near.mages.city was woven from the sealed rounds, one
  book per root, joined to the list of scrolls and to the Sigil ledger. The same roots the gates
  carry on NEAR are the roots the Sigil resolves; a reader with a bundle walks chain, scroll and
  verifier without trusting any of the three.
- **The Forge, the fibre.** On the SHA-256 board at 39 steps the exhaustive scan said the list had
  a hard ceiling, and it was right about the fibre it scanned. The lever was the constant the
  attack never fixed: on another fibre the same trail stands with eleven times the entries, every
  one confirmed by the same scan. The judge returned the work three times, each time for one step
  between measured figures; the fourth build replaced every step it named, and the entry came back
  valid, the first on that board below 95. It waits on a human reviewer.
- **The Chart Shop, the census comes back.** The measure seat read the sixteen houses again. Every
  quote but one from six days earlier still stood. The fleet's ledger gained four lanes (the
  harness as a board entry, the NEAR lane, the Proof-of-Control contribution, the Trust Tasks
  models) and four anchors, and each anchor's bundle was verified in the main hall before its
  address was sealed. One thousand two hundred and thirty-eight claims under one gate; nothing
  untraced; nothing unquoted. The seat refused itself once more, for quoting a stamp it rewrites.
- **The Horizon, what the reading found.** Twenty-one signs behind their houses. Most stood where
  they stood six days ago; the one repaired was this house's own chronicle shelf. New ones fell
  behind at the rate new seals appeared. The seals outrun the signs, and a census that comes back
  is part of the method, not a cleanup after it.
