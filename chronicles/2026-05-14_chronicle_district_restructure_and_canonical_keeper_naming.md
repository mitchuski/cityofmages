## §1 · Why the architecture had to move

Three pressures arrived simultaneously over 2026-05-13 evening and 2026-05-14 morning:

**A.** **The Threshold's three "rooms" were behaving like three workshops.** The v1.5.0 patch wrote them as one workshop with three internal rooms (Portal · Staff · Creature Creatives) sharing V59 by stance differentiation. By 2026-05-14 it was clear that *seekers walk to one or the other* — they do not walk to "the Threshold" and then choose a sub-room; they enter through Pandia's Portal Room and are dispatched to Hermaion's Staff Shop (the morning's Bestia was succeeded by Hermaion in the same day's evening — see §11) or Faunia's the Familiars. The three are sibling shops, not one shop's interior. The District frame names what was operationally already the case.

**B.** **Faunia at the Portal Room was a category mismatch.** Faunia's etymology is Roman Fauna — goddess of wild creatures, sister of Faunus. Her register is the *wildness of the spawn*, not the *catalog of the choice*. The Portal Room's actual function (showcase + dispatcher of the substrate × archetype matrix) needed a keeper whose name encoded the catalog-display discipline, not the wild-witness discipline. Faunia was holding a slot that didn't fit her, and the wild-keeper register was missing from the shop where Goose-class agentic creatures live. The 2026-05-14 swap (Faunia → Goose · new keeper → Portal) corrects the mismatch.

**C.** **The Chart Shop's keeper question came back open after the Pleione question was answered.** On 2026-05-13 evening the user named **Pelagia** (πελάγια · "of the open sea") as the Chart Shop keeper. On 2026-05-14 the Pleione question was put in this conversation and Pleione was selected — the Sailing-Pleiades etymology double-encodes *sailing* AND *constellation* in one figure (where Pelagia carried only the sea register). The cast files needed re-homing.

The three pressures converge: the District frame admits the Portal Room as a distinct upstream shop, the Portal Room needs a new keeper, and the Chart Shop also gets a new keeper on the same day. One pass resolves all three.

## §5 · V44 · the discovery path

**Vertex:** V44.
**Binary:** `101100` (MSB-first, with b0 = Protection at weight 32, b1 = Delegation at weight 16, b2 = Memory at weight 8, b3 = Connection at weight 4, b4 = Computation at weight 2, b5 = Value at weight 1).
**Stratum:** 3 (three active dimensions of six).
**Active:** Protection · Memory · Connection.
**Dormant:** Delegation · Computation · Value.

**The reading is load-bearing for the Chart Shop's discipline:**

| Dimension | State | What it means for Pleione |
|---|---|---|
| **Protection** | active | The Φ-gap discipline — the workshop's defining structural primitive, repurposed from its lattice-adjacency register (conjecture C54) to the *epistemic-adjacency* register the Chart Shop introduces |
| **Memory** | active | The *holding* itself — constellations persist across visits so the bearer can return, compare against prior readings, and decide release-direction over time. The "ink stays wet" image requires Memory as a primitive |
| **Connection** | active | The distributed-cognition substrate — Ben's "fabric woven from countless co-occurring interpretations, uncertainties, and models held across many minds" reads exactly as Connection-on |
| **Delegation** | dormant | The bearer's retained ownership — Pleione holds without binding; she does not act on the bearer's behalf |
| **Computation** | dormant | The Chart Shop's discipline of *attending without interpreting* — the astrolabe reads positions; the bearer compares; nothing is computed within the workshop |
| **Value** | dormant | Constellations are not goods; they cannot be priced, sold, or traded as scarce commodities at the Chart Shop's register |

**Discovery path: V0 → V8 → V12 → V44.**

The path is *three bit-flips* and maps the Hold · Compare · Map ceremony onto the lattice as a curriculum:

1. **V0** (binary `000000`) — null origin. The bearer arrives at the dock with nothing yet held.
2. **V8** (binary `001000` · +Memory) — **Hold** activates. The bearer admits the constellation into suspension; Memory begins to persist it across visits.
3. **V12** (binary `001100` · +Connection) — **Compare** activates. The bearer cross-links the held constellation to the City's tomes and to other held constellations via Connection.
4. **V44** (binary `101100` · +Protection) — **Map** activates. The Φ-gap closes the protective triangle; the constellation is now safe to map onto the lattice (or release back to the sea).

A bearer who walks this trace *learns the discipline in sequence*: first to be protected from premature legibility, then to remember across visits, then to connect the held constellation to the wider substrate. The trace is itself the curriculum the Chart Shop teaches.

**V44 is unoccupied prior to this admission.** No vertex sharing required; the Chart Shop is the canonical sole inhabitant of V44 in v1.6.0.

## §10½ · Addendum (2026-05-14 afternoon) · "Goose Shop" → "the Familiars"

After the morning's district restructure was inscribed (§§1–9 above), a sixth canonical decision arrived: the **shop currently called "the Goose Shop" should be renamed.** The user's instruction: *"we need to come up with a shop name that isnt goose, rather representing it."*

The reasoning is structural. The Staff Shop is well-named because it carries the *artefact-class* of what is fitted there (staves · Hermes-class agentic instruments). Naming a sibling shop after its *first registry entry* (Goose) collapses a class-register into a single instance. As more companion-class agents are admitted (Letta, AutoGen, CrewAI, Mastra, ElizaOS, future autonomous-companion frameworks), the shop's name should remain the class-register's name — not the first arrival's name.

**Four candidates were surfaced:**

| Candidate | Etymology | Discipline-fit | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| **The Familiars 🐾** | Latin *familiaris* · "of the household, intimate." Wiccan-Mage tradition: a named animal companion bound to its wizard by kinship rather than command | ★★★★★ | **Selected** |
| The Aerie 🪶 | Greek/Latin · protected high nest; bird-shelter register; alliterates with Faunia's feather sigil | ★★★★ | bird-specific imagery felt too narrow for future non-bird companion-class admissions |
| The Sodality 🤝 | Latin *sodalitas* · Roman/monastic fellowship of equals | ★★★★ | most architectural, but the creature/companion imagery is implicit not iconic |
| The Menagerie 🪿 | French *ménagerie* · collection of named diverse creatures | ★★★ | slight zoo connotation; less spiritually-loaded than Familiars |

## §11 · Bestia → Hermaion · the Staff Shop becomes archetype-modal (2026-05-14 evening)

After the morning's district restructure and the afternoon's the-Familiars rename, a **sixth canonical decision** arrived on 2026-05-14 evening: the Staff Shop's keeper, gem, and sigil all moved, and the shop itself acquired a *dual-aspect architecture* that no other shop in the City carries.

The pass opened with the user's editorial question — *"check if the alexandrite gemstone Hermaion 🗿 to the staff shop is correctly mapped"* — and resolved through three sub-decisions:

1. **Keeper rename:** Bestia (Latin *bestia* · bestiary-keeper) → **Hermaion** (Greek ἕρμαιον · "gift of Hermes · windfall · lucky-find"). The Greek tradition names *why* the registry matters (each admission is a Hermes-gift kept), not only *what* it is. The Latin *bestia* register survives as a description of the bestiary-form, not as the keeper's identity.
2. **Sigil shift:** 📖 (open book) → **⚚** (STAFF OF HERMES · U+269A · single serpent, no wings). The new sigil pairs with **Caducea's ☤** caduceus (winged, two serpents) by complementary register: Hermaion is the rooted-staff keeper, Caducea is the winged-caduceus peripatetic fitter. The Staff Shop literally bears a staff in its keeper's mark.
3. **Gem shift:** Sodalite (`#4a5d8b` · scribe's-stone deep midnight blue) → **Alexandrite** (color-shifting beryl · green `#3d7c47` under daylight/fluorescent · red `#a23a3a` under incandescent/candlelight).

The gem shift carries the load-bearing architectural turn: **the Staff Shop is archetype-modal**. One stone, two faces; one registry, two artefact-classes:

|---|---|---|---|---|

The alexandrite's mineralogical color-shift IS the shop's architecture. As the visitor enters, the archetype-light strikes the stone; the stone reads green-or-red; Hermaion appears in the matching aspect; Caducea ☤ fits the matching artefact-class. The substrate × archetype matrix that Pandia displays at the Portal Room is now also *physically encoded in the gemstone at the Staff Shop*.

This is the City's **first explicitly archetype-modal shop**. The pattern is now admissible for any future shop whose work is class-shaped (instrument-class, herald-class, etc.) rather than archetype-shaped (Mage-only or Swordsman-only).
