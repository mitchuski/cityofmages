## §0 · What this chronicle is

The record of a binding pass: on 2026-05-13, Tomes I, II, and III of the Second Person Spellbook were translated from their indexed-research-note form (catalogued at `agentprivacy-docs/SECOND_PERSON_TOMES_INDEX_v1.md` on 2026-05-09) into narrative-act files in the Tome IV / Tome V tradition. Twenty-four acts in total — six for Tome I (Greek-letter numbered α through ζ), seven for Tome II, eleven for Tome III. Bound at `cityofmages/tomes/tome-i-the-convergence/`, `tome-ii-the-lyapunov/`, and `tome-iii-selenes-witness/`. Mirrored to `agentprivacy_master/docs/tomes/` for the Next.js loader. Rendered on the `/tomes` page with three new sections positioned before Tome IV.

The pass was editorial-translation rather than content-authoring. Each act's content already existed in the research-note corpus; the binding rendered the content in second-person address with proverb, third-person cast voice, vertex declaration, honesty labels, and closing inscription per the Tome IV/V template.

## §3 · Form discipline

Each act follows the canonical narrative-act form established by Tome IV/V:

- **Frontmatter** with spellbook, tome, act, title, status, length_words, voice, cast, ring_position, teaches, v6_lineage (where applicable), source_material, honesty_label, license, signature, tome_status (where applicable)
- **H1**: `# Tome [N] — *Title*`
- **H2**: `## Act [N] — *Title*`
- **Proverb** in blockquote at the top of the body
- **Narrative body** in second person reader / third person cast, ~750–880 words per act, no em-dashes per house-style discipline
- **Compression** — one-paragraph summary
- **Proverb** — repeated for citation discipline
- **Confidence** — operational / architectural / conjectural labels per the corpus's honesty discipline
- **Cross-references** — companion acts, v6 lineage, external citations
- **Author note** — meta paragraph
- **Optional [[rpp:]] line** — the relationship proverb protocol marker
- **Closing inscription** `(⚔️⊥⿻⊥🧙)😊` (with Aletheia/Lethe `𝓐⿻𝓠` variant for Tome III acts that reference the complement-pair register)

The form was preserved across all twenty-four acts.

This chronicle records the completion of that pass.
