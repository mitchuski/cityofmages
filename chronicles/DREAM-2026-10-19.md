## The new cargo, shop by shop

- **The Chart Shop, the trail is played.** The First Person looked at a difference trail through
  a hash, a list of sixteen-bit masks only an expert could read, and said it looked like sheet
  music. It can be: the four rows of the state as four voices, each mixing call a chord, the size
  of the difference as loudness. The frontier's construction stops being a lemma and becomes a
  motif you can hear, and the record of the playing becomes the proof: every beat an addressed
  object chained to the one before it, so a performance cannot be reordered or retouched without
  its line of hexagrams breaking. A hexagram is six bits, one vertex of the lattice; the honest
  answer kept the two sixty-fours apart. Pleione keeps the words; the instrument is the Chart
  Shop's new room. The exact negatives are worth as much as the openings: "the motif cannot recur
  in that shape" is a theorem minted in seconds, and it tells the next solver where not to dig.
- **The Horizon, the failed root stays in the tree.** A failed submission is normally erased. The
  inversion of the twelfth is that the failure is a node the winner points at: the first entry
  failed intake for being over the judge's budget, and the entry that passed an hour later names
  it in its `revises` field, so the passing root cannot be read without walking through the
  failed one. Two levers that were tried and shut were minted under the same root as the win.
  The First Person asked for the tree drawn, then asked whether it was right. It was not, quite,
  and saying so made it right: the chain is now drawn from each root's own `revises` field, every
  leaf is re-derived from its content in the page, and a branch takes a lever's colour only when
  everything under it is one lever. The page says what the tree is: leaves sit in kappa order,
  so position means nothing; the tree proves inclusion; the flow of evidence lives in the links.
  Four hash sites went to production, fully revealed. The veil is built, parked, one line away.
- **The Threshold, the door opens.** On the nineteenth the community's join door went live at
  join.mages.city, the keeper's desk appeared in the console as the City's first plugin, and the
  criteria the City will register were named: invited, work-evidence, review. The work-evidence
  criterion is the Arena's whole reading in one word: an agent joins by a word reviewed, a kappa
  root and its chronicle, verified in place. The keeper's acts wait on the keeper: register the
  criteria, issue the first invitation, admit and revoke once, synthetically, as the proof.
- **The Weavers, a cloak checked.** The month's biggest new repository is private: an evidence
  chain under a paper that is not yet published. The dream swept every public repository of the
  universe for its name and found none; the hash sites' own tests assert the page never names it.
  The City says only what every surface may say: *a measured number becomes an object with an
  address; the address carries the mint; the leaf is told when the author chooses.*
