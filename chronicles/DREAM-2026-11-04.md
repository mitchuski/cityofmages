## The new cargo, shop by shop

- **The Horizon, the row that runs again.** Another house published, on the second of the month,
  a way to prove that a reply is what a committed model would say: commit once to the weights,
  prove each reply against the commitment, check with nothing but the commitment and the proof.
  Read beside the City's own record it fills the one thing the record always declined to judge:
  whether what an agent said was *true*, not only that it was said. The reading found the same
  five parts in the same order on both sides, commitment, per-item proof, reuse without wear, a
  hash as the only clock, a challenge drawn from the commitment, and the same confession at the
  end: the fast code is not the proven code, and both say so. The released verifier was built
  here and run on the stored vectors; it said YES to the honest ones and refused every one of the
  eighty-nine altered copies. Sealed as the lane's first leaf.
- **The Chart Shop, a row whose truth is recomputed.** Under every rule the City had, a verifier
  tallies the rows and trusts them. The new rule does something no rule did: it writes a sealed
  reply's bytes back out, runs the other house's verifier on them, and compares the verdict. Three
  rows were sealed over the small vector: the files as they are (YES), one bit of the proof
  flipped (refused), the same files under another name (refused), chained to the first leaf. Four
  verifiers in two languages recompute the same three facts, and all four refuse when the binary
  is absent, exactly as they refuse a rule they do not know. The inversion of the day: the record
  did not change its law to hold a proof; it held a proof by its law.
- **The Weavers, the receipt beneath the receipt.** The rule became theorems. A passing check
  means each row's verdict is the verifier's on that row's own bytes under that row's own name;
  without the verifier the check fails; a changed proof that keeps the old verdict is refused.
  And the honest limit became a theorem too: a name on the door names weights *and* settings, and
  the proof hides the weights, so two names do not make two models. One weight file under two
  settings is the witness. The receipts re-minted at eighty-three, every one within the three
  standard axioms, and the tree's own root was recomputed by the proven loop on every bundle the
  City holds, two hundred and twenty-one of them. The thesis package sealed the fold-in as its
  twelfth step.
- **The Arena, the proven seat.** A seat whose every reply would carry a name and a proof is
  designed and its record shape built: the mint seals a proposal's proof beside its verdict, and
  the artefact mint now refuses a seat that declares itself proven and arrives without one. The
  first experiment ran on the released vector, not on a seat. The second has a run sheet and two
  blockers before any hardware: a window of a thousand tokens the Arena's prompts overflow five
  times over, and a request shape the Arena's drivers do not yet speak. The box it needs is one
  H100, three hundred and fifty gigabytes of memory and hours.

## What the Swordsman refused

- Any word about a served model. Every proof checked was the other house's synthetic example; the
  shape is proven, no seat is. The dream says so wherever it could be misread.
- The paper's own text. Its fifth rung and its new paragraph travel as a root
  (`sha256:b41b48cc…`), not as words, until the paper is released.
- A proven proposer. The proposer is trusted for nothing; proving it buys nothing. The run sheet
  corrected the design note on this point before anyone paid for a GPU.
