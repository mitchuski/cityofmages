## 1. Give practice somewhere to happen

The request was to participate before trying to dominate the board. Establish an honestly attributed, below-par entry on each substantive challenge, then return for deeper improvements. Hello, World! belongs on the practice green. It teaches a method but does not count as a full hole.

The catalogue is recorded in zkgolf_mage's campaign. Its course metaphor names a place to practise, not a new theorem, registry identity or bound City district. The longer telling is proposed as **The Proof Links: A Tome of Practice**. No vertex or new keeper has been assigned.

## 2. Keep the flag where it was

Soulbae 🧙 proposes a cheaper route. Soulbis ⚔️ checks the artifact against the same trusted destination. The First Person authorizes contribution. Frozen bytes, exact statements, cost proofs, witness requirements and the permitted axiom closure connect the roles to actual work.

The contexts share a machine and model family. Their separation is procedural; no statistical independence or information-theoretic privacy bound has been established. The Lean kernel and official comparator carry the correctness claim.

## 3. Let the first strokes be small

BLAKE3's first accepted contribution removed redundant final-output copies. The tutorial used seven witnessed low bits and a constrained residual to represent a byte's high bit. Each passed local verification and server acceptance. Neither set a new public record.

The next hole is fixed-base secp256k1. The reference is under assay; the proposer is exploring transfer of the tutorial's residual byte check into coordinate serialization. That is a proposal, not another score.

## 4. Bring the spellbook, keep its meanings distinct

The star sigil supplies a concrete fingerprint and a lesson in recomputation. Its glyph comparison counts changes in hexadecimal characters; lattice-neighbour bit flips and internal SHA carries are different objects. FLOCK supplies carry-product algebra. Earlier Lean work supplies explicit axiom and evidence discipline. None becomes a cheaper circuit merely by entering the story.

The public SHA leader already contains the obvious copy and carry improvements. Its historical source lacks obligations in today's pinned interface and uses an older witness API. A future residual-carry experiment is written down, with the requirement to count both consumers and the producer. There is no ready record candidate from that inspection.

## 5. Leave room to return

The desktop hosts one heavy build at a time, reusing existing dependencies. The SHA attempt crossed no resource stop. Hardware sponsorship remains a set of options, not a purchase or a measured necessity. Pausing should preserve a reproducible next stroke rather than leave a machine filling unattended.

The course's book therefore carries accepted strokes, queued cards and failed experiments separately. A future mage can inherit the source and its limits together.

## The course opens onto the DTG workshop

The First Person made the deeper connection explicit: the golf course is one approachable example of a much larger community capability. The ZK Book can guide the course and the work beyond it. Clean and Lean offer a pathway to specify, compose and formally check circuits for purpose-specific presentations of DTG credentials and trust-graph relationships.

The proposed agentic compiler begins with a reviewed predicate card: what is proved, whose evidence grounds it, which inputs are public, which witness stays private, the adversary and the time horizon. It produces a formal relation, circuit, witness program, proof obligations and a reproducible export. A community's independent run and deployment decision follow; no golf receipt substitutes for them.

The inspected Clean guide demonstrates specification-first circuit development and an explicit constraint/witness export path. The inspected DTG task-force practice already supplies cards, runtimes, fixtures and a registry boundary. Connecting them is a design proposal, not a claim that this integration is running. The first proposed slice is a scoped community-role presentation from an authenticated membership snapshot, with holder binding, freshness and transcript boundaries written down.

The City and mages.city plan now names this pathway in `mages-city/DTG_AGENTIC_CIRCUIT_PATHWAY.md`, with a planning mirror in the board working repository's `docs/`. The course trains reusable techniques. The forge fulfils specific community proof commissions. The board coordinates requests, reproducible evidence and review. The holder controls disclosure, and the relying party retains its policy decision.

An accepted issuer attestation is not a proof that its real-world judgment was correct. A valid graph-membership proof is not proof that the root is current or authoritative. Lean correctness is not by itself a proof of deployment-level unlinkability. Those boundaries are part of the requested compiler contract, not notes to add after optimization.
