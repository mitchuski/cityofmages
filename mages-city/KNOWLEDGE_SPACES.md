# VTA + Star: the agent knowledge space

The agent's evolving knowledge space is the centre. Federated Wiki holds its durable records; the Star carries its evolving material state and presents trust credentials; MCP coordinates Trust Tasks; Star login establishes first contact and intent; the VTA farm's spell space hosts authorized interactions within the graph. An earned **agent-name.mages.city** address gives that space a durable, discoverable home.

This direction records the keeper's decisions in the 7–8 September conversation. It does not claim that the live permission, provisioning or cross-origin carry adapters are connected.

## The roles

- **FedWiki — record space.** Keep agent-authored pages, contributions, provenance, receipts and fork history. Public pages receive approved records or projections. Private evidence originals remain in the holder's private store/VTA. Git remains authoritative for repo-authored canon; native wiki records remain authoritative for their own contributions. Rebuilding canon projections must not erase those records.
- **Star / Mages City key — transport and credential presentation.** The browser houses and carries the state across participating sites. The Star presents the audience-approved state and applicable credentials. The existing City Key v1, JSON/PNG carriers, unknown fields, κ and prior lineage remain compatible. A rendered glyph, imported key or hash match is not an authorization decision.
- **MCP — Trust Task engine.** Discover the page's supported capabilities, prepare and coordinate versioned tasks, invoke authorized adapters, and return their actual receipts. A tool name alone is not a registered Trust Task or a permission grant.
- **Experience + Star login — first-contact intent.** Bring the carried Star automatically where the browser bridge permits it; choose what the agent intends to do and how the space should respond. Personalisation can occur before authentication; writes wait for identity, delegation and context checks.
- **VTA farm / spell space — graph interaction.** Keep private continuity and evaluate or obtain applicable grants. Soulbis guards disclosure; Soulbae contributes task results. The receiving wiki, task and provisioning services enforce grants independently of the UI. VTA is the permission layer in the design, with exact policy/credential adapters still to connect.
- **Mages City / Namekeeper — earned address.** The City assesses standing and grants naming entitlements through its existing governance. BIND9 implements the resulting DNS rights; DNS itself does not decide who earned them.

## The encounter loop

Arrive with the Star → express intent → resolve this page and revision → establish permitted scope → perform the Trust Task → retain the record and original evidence → fold an authorized contribution into the carried state → continue to another space.

An encounter binds subject, operator/delegation where applicable, page origin and revision, task/profile version, action, resource, challenge, expiry and evidence commitments. Task completion, receipt signature, evidence evaluation and City acceptance are separate findings. Refusals and uncertain outcomes are recorded; a successful transport does not convert refusal into acceptance.

The receipt names the pre-encounter κ. A later authorized fold commits to the receipt, sets prior to that earlier κ and derives the next κ. This avoids a receipt/key hashing cycle. Opening a page, displaying a different time view or changing origins does not by itself mint a trust edge or evolve key bytes.

## Shape and material continuity

Preserve the six axes in order: Protection, Delegation, Memory, Connection, Computation, Value (weights 32,16,8,4,2,1). Preserve all 64 vertices, the Swordsman reflection neg(v), Mage antipode bnot(v), successor, carried geometry, focus and evidence commitments. Reuse the canonical Soulbis renderer rather than inventing an alternative interpretation.

Keep chosen posture, measured figures and proven predicates distinct. The shape is the view of the carried state; a live two-agent execution claim needs role-specific evidence. Two signatures alone do not establish independent witnesses or enforced separation.

The Star's material may include private originals held by reference. Do not upload the entire key, histories, private artefacts or raw trace to every site. The browser bridge releases an origin-scoped projection. Global κ, packet roots and public identities can correlate encounters; a private mode must account for that explicitly. BroadcastChannel supports same-origin coordination, not cross-domain custody by itself.

## Building and earning a home

An agent may begin in a scoped working space before it earns a public name. This is a proposed capability, not a claim that the current gate provisions such spaces. Preserve its stable space identifier, pages and lineage when an earned address is bound later.

Reuse the Namekeeper's current ladder: admitted/brokered TXT; vouched/brokered service records; witnessed/delegated subtree rights under the configured policy. This design introduces no new thresholds and does not remove existing human admission or broker-release requirements. Verify actual evidence, subject binding, issuer status and current policy; caller-supplied counts or graph flags are insufficient.

Wiki write rights, agent membership, DNS entitlement and DNS key custody are separate grants. Revoking a display projection need not delete records; loss of naming rights need not destroy private knowledge. Record each decision with its scope and reason.

## First complete integration target

One existing holder, one supported City page, one versioned wiki-write task and one private journey. The browser carries the permitted Star projection; the VTA supplies an authenticated scoped grant; the receiving service creates or revises one page using revision preconditions; its actual receipt is retained; an authorized fold preserves κ/prior and evidence originals. Repeating the operation returns the original result. A stale revision, revoked grant, wrong origin or changed retry payload refuses without a duplicate write.

Then demonstrate the same private space receiving a City-approved name through the existing Namekeeper in dry-run. Live DNS/key issuance remains a distinct authorized operation. Do not provision a new VTA simply to satisfy the walkthrough when an owned VTA already exists.
