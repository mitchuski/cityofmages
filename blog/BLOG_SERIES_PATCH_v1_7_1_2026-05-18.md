## §0 · TL;DR

The 12 blog posts in this directory were drafted on **2026-05-09**, when the City of Mages corpus stood at **grimoire v1.2.3**. Since then, the corpus has moved through **eight pinned/patch milestones** (v1.2.4 → v1.4.0 → v1.5.0 → v1.5.1 → v1.6.0 PINNED → v1.7.0 → v1.7.1 PINNED) and the public IPFS grimoire now resolves at **`bafybeibr35xfasepuvfti4dnwiiig6gidaf5sffvjf4rnhrlpqcke3ivy4`** as of 2026-05-17.

What was a city of **9 producer-shops + 1 Temple + 1 Bonfire** when the posts were drafted is now a **16-workshop city with two named districts, an eighth-element monument (the Tower), a Register of Invitations, and 8 open Tomes**. The structural-relationship taxonomy grew from 3 kindred-X categories to **5**. The cast-tier ladder grew from 6 tiers to **7**. The mana taxonomy was admitted as **four operational axes**.

This document does three things:

1. **§1–§2 · Analysis.** What the drafts assumed; what has shifted; where the drafts now drift against canon.
2. **§3 · Per-post edit suggestions.** Specific minimum-touch edits to each of the 12 drafted posts so they read coherently against v1.7.1 without rewriting them.
3. **§4–§6 · Forward weave.** A reframe of the Movement Three structure (posts 13–16), plus proposed new posts for the season's admissions (Solchanting, the Threshold District, the Chart Shop, the Tower, the Fourth Turn), and the tome/story coherence weave that ties the series to the Tome corpus.

The voice and lineage of the drafts is sound. The work below is **patching**, not rewriting.
