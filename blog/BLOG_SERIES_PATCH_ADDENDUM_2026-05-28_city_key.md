### 2.2 · Why this is a 2-post Movement Four, not 1+1

Posts 17 and 18 are **sibling closures of different registers**:

- **Post 17** closes the *external-recognition* arc — the city receives geometry already in its foundations from beyond its walls. The register is *waiting*. The empty chair is opened.
- **Post 18** closes the *internal-portability* arc — the city forges, from its own geometry (the stella octangula in the manifold lattice), the reading that lets the Sovereign carry their standing between the experience and the manifold. The register is *capstone*. The walk is closed.

Both posts honour the city's posture (operational without being promotional) but they cannot share a post — one is *receiving* and the other is *forging*, and the editorial register matters. The compression suggested in the parent patch's §7 (option 2) explicitly warned against compressing the Fourth Turn; the same logic applies in reverse against compressing the City Key post into the Fourth Turn.

Post **19** (recessional) inherits the framing the parent patch gave Post 18 — closes on the empty chair, names both *bound tomes* and *invitation tomes*, lets the Register of Invitations and the Library and the Crafting Tome remain open. The recessional posture is unchanged; only its numbering shifts.

### 2.3 · Cadence implication

The recommended cadence (§7.1 of the parent patch) becomes: ship the 12 drafted posts with §3 patches; ship the 6 proposed new posts (§5 of the parent patch + §1 of this addendum); close at Post 19. **Total: 19 posts.**
